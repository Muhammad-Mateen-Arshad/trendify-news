Pakistan summons Indian envoy over killing of 2 civilians by border troops

Published by AI Pipeline | October 03, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan's Foreign Office summoned the Indian chargé d’affaires on Saturday to lodge a strong protest over the killing of two unarmed Pakistani civilians by Indian Border Security Force (BSF) personnel. The shooting occurred on Friday in the Bedian sector along the international border near Kasur, leaving two citizens dead and a third wounded.

Key facts

  • The deceased were identified as Azmat Rasool and Mohsin Jutt, both residents of Jattan Wali Haveli in Ladhar village. The injured individual was transported for medical care.
  • Security sources noted that although the men moved slightly past a border pillar, none attempted to cross the border fence. The unprovoked firing was described as a breach of border standard operating procedures.
  • Pakistan's Foreign Office rejected claims labeling the victims as infiltrators, declaring the action disproportionate, unjustified, and a serious breach of international law and human rights.
  • Pakistan demanded that India conduct an immediate, transparent, and credible inquiry, take legal and disciplinary action against those responsible, and formally communicate the findings to Pakistani officials.
  • The Foreign Office urged India to respect border procedures and exercise restraint to prevent similar events.
  • A statement or reaction from Indian officials was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the border incident occurred a day after Inter-Services Public Relations Director General Lt Gen Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry held a press conference rejecting Indian claims about neutralizing an alleged terrorist tied to the Pakistani military. Addressing questions about possible military escalation, Lt Gen Chaudhry stated that Pakistan's armed forces are ready for any move and warned that retribution would be severe.
