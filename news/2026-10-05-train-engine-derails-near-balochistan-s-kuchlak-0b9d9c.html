Train engine derails near Balochistan's Kuchlak

Published on October 05, 2026

What happened

The engine of the newly introduced Jinnah Peoples Train derailed near the Kuchlak Railway Station on Sunday while departing toward Quetta, according to Dawn. No injuries or casualties occurred during the incident, and every passenger on board remained safe. Following a short delay, railway personnel provided a substitute engine, enabling the service to proceed to Sariab.

Key facts

  • The derailment involved engine No. 5120 on Line No. 3 while it was reversing during regular shunting tasks.
  • The Jinnah Peoples Train is a recently launched passenger line operating between Kuchlak and Sariab.
  • Railway officials initially attributed the derailment to the suspected theft of a fishplate, noting that an investigation is in progress.
  • Kuchlak police rejected the railway administration's account, stating the train derailed because of poor track conditions where workers had been performing maintenance.
  • Railway management has formally instructed an inquiry to confirm the actual cause of the event.
  • The exact count of passengers on board was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

The incident highlights a disagreement between law enforcement and railway authorities regarding safety hazards on the active line. While railway officials pointed to stolen hardware, local police cited bad track conditions during ongoing labor. Prompt substitution of the engine prevented extended travel disruptions for passengers on the new route.
