Makkah defence pact meeting to be held today in Riyadh, confirms FO

Published on October 05, 2026

According to Dawn, Pakistan’s Foreign Office has confirmed that the Strategic Political and Defence Committee of the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement is holding a meeting in Riyadh. The session brings together key leadership from Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Türkiye to review regional security developments and evaluate ongoing military and political cooperation under the defense framework. Prior to arriving in Riyadh, Pakistan's Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar visited Madina to offer prayers at the Prophet’s Mosque.

What happened

According to Dawn, the emergency session was organized to focus on recent Houthi attacks directed at Saudi Arabia. The regional conflict has escalated after Yemen’s Saudi-led coalition initiated a military campaign to retake territory occupied by the Houthis. Recent fighting included strikes by Saudi Arabia on Yemen’s capital, Sanaa, which were followed by a Houthi missile and drone assault targeting an Aramco facility south of Riyadh. Hostilities expanded after Houthi forces seized control of Yemen’s Red Sea coastline and the Bab al-Mandeb strait.

Key facts

According to Dawn, key elements of the pact and the session include:

  • Pakistan’s delegation is composed of Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir, and Defence Minister Khawaja Asif.
  • The Foreign Ministers, Defence Ministers, and Chiefs of General Staff from Saudi Arabia and Türkiye are also attending the session.
  • Formed on August 7, the defense pact functions on mutual deterrence, treating an attack on one signatory as an attack against all member states.
  • Pakistan is managing the alliance's secretariat for an initial three-year term, with retired Lieutenant General Nauman Mahmood serving as the first secretary general. The first committee session took place in Istanbul on August 31, followed by a military chiefs' conference in Riyadh on September 25.
  • Specific joint operational strategies from the meeting were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Pakistan's participation reflects its commitment to contributing to peace and stability across the region. Pakistani leaders, including Inter-Services Public Relations Director General Lieutenant General Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry and Defence Minister Khawaja Asif, have expressed unconditional support for Saudi Arabia, stating that Pakistan will stand by the kingdom both diplomatically and practically if conflict with Houthi rebels intensifies.
