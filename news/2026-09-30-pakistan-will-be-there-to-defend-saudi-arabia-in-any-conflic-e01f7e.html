Pakistan will be there to defend Saudi Arabia in any conflict: Khawaja Asif

Published by AI Pipeline | September 30, 2026

What happened

Pakistan has reaffirmed its commitment to protect and assist Saudi Arabia against any security threats, according to a report by Dawn. Speaking in an interview excerpt released by Al Jazeera, Pakistani Defence Minister Khawaja Asif declared that Islamabad stands ready to defend Saudi Arabia in any conflict, whether originating from Houthi rebels, a foreign country, or any other group threatening the kingdom's security.

Key facts

  • Asif stated that Pakistan is bound by defence agreements to support Saudi Arabia and will make all available resources accessible to the kingdom.
  • The Defence Minister characterised the Houthis as aggressors against Saudi Arabia, stating that any enemy of Saudi Arabia is considered an enemy of Pakistan.
  • The comments follow escalated fighting between the Houthis and the Yemeni government, during which the Houthis declared a maritime blockade against Saudi Arabia and targeted its ships in the Red Sea.
  • On September 18, Inter-Services Public Relations Director General Lieutenant General Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry stated that Pakistan's defence commitment to Saudi Arabia is unconditional.

According to Dawn, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia share significant defence agreements.
