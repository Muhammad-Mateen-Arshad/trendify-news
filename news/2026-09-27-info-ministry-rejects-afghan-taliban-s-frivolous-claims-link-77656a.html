Info ministry rejects Afghan Taliban's 'frivolous' claims linking Pakistan to reported Nuristan violence

Published by AI Pipeline | September 27, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan's Information Ministry has formally rejected allegations by the Afghan Taliban regime claiming Pakistani security forces were involved in internal clashes within Afghanistan's Nuristan province. The Afghan defense ministry had previously asserted that individuals supported by Pakistan crossed the border to participate in the reported violence. In response, Pakistan's information ministry issued a statement through its official fact-check account on X, calling the accusations completely fabricated, frivolous, and devoid of truth.

Key facts

  • Denial of involvement: Pakistan denied that its military or intelligence agencies engaged in clashes in Nuristan or elsewhere in Afghanistan, or that they recruited, trained, or facilitated former Afghan personnel or militant groups like Daesh.
  • Absence of proof: The ministry stated that Afghan authorities failed to provide any supporting evidence, such as official documents, communications, forensic data, or independent verification.
  • Dismissal of social media footage: Officials rejected a video circulating online as evidence, characterizing it as a staged recording featuring unarmed and improperly equipped individuals.
  • Counter-accusations: Pakistan urged the Taliban administration to address internal security and dissent directly, and to fulfill commitments preventing Afghan soil from being used for terrorism against neighboring countries.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the disagreement highlights ongoing border tensions and strained diplomatic ties between the two neighbors over cross-border security. Islamabad has repeatedly called on Afghan authorities to dismantle militant hideouts, particularly those linked to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP). A UN Security Council-mandated monitoring report confirmed that ongoing support for the TTP inside Afghanistan has raised the frequency and severity of attacks inside Pakistan.

Recent border conflicts have involved cross-border firing, Pakistani airstrikes under Operation Ghazab lil Haq targeting militant hideouts, and drone shootdowns along the frontier.
