Govt reduces petrol price by Rs2.27 per litre, high-speed diesel rate by Rs3.56

Published by AI Pipeline | September 29, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, the government has reduced domestic fuel prices, lowering the price of petrol by Rs2.27 per litre and high-speed diesel (HSD) by Rs3.56 per litre. A notification issued by the Petroleum Division indicates that the revised rates are effective starting Sept 29.

Under the new pricing structure, petrol will retail at Rs389.03 per litre, while HSD will cost Rs404.97 per litre. The government continues to levy Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel.

Key facts

  • According to Dawn, both fuel types previously experienced significant price peaks on April 3, with HSD reaching Rs520.35 per litre and petrol hitting Rs458.41 per litre following global market surges tied to the US-Iran conflict.
  • On July 17, Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik announced that the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (Ogra) would fix fuel rates daily rather than weekly due to international market fluctuations.
  • To manage energy demands amid ongoing regional conflicts, the government reintroduced austerity rules requiring commercial markets to close by 9pm and reducing official vehicle fuel allocations by 50 per cent for three months.
  • Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif announced a relief scheme on September 13 specifically for drivers of motorcycles, autos, and vehicles with engine capacities up to 800cc.

Why it matters

As reported by Dawn, fuel pricing directly affects economic activity and household expenses. Petrol is heavily utilized in private vehicles, two-wheelers, and rickshaws, making changes impactful for middle and lower-middle-class consumers. HSD powers heavy transport, large generators, and power stations, influencing broader public costs.

Petrol and diesel represent major government revenue streams, generating monthly sales of roughly 700,000 to 800,000 tonnes, compared to kerosene demand of 10,000 tonnes monthly. Full operational details of the subsidised fuel mechanisms are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
