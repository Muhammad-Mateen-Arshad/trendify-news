The Boox Palma 3 gets stylus support and a sleek redesign

🕒 Published on September 16, 2026 at 06:07 AM | Auto-Generated AI Log

Onyx Boox has officially unveiled the Boox Palma 3, giving tech enthusiasts and dedicated bookworms a fresh reason to look twice at smartphone-sized E Ink devices. Coming nearly two years after the launch of the popular Palma 2, this latest iteration transforms the beloved pocket e-reader into a far more versatile digital companion. While the previous generation earned a loyal following for its ultra-portable form factor, the Palma 3 raises the bar with a striking visual refresh, enhanced performance, and a headline feature that fans have requested for years: active stylus support.

A Sleek Redesign Meets Pocket Productivity

The most immediate change lies in the device’s aesthetics. The Boox Palma 3 boasts a sleek, modernized redesign that feels considerably more premium in the hand. Slimmer bezels, refined edge contouring, and an upgraded tactile back panel give it the look and feel of a high-end flagship smartphone, minus the eye-straining OLED glow. However, the true game-changer is the addition of stylus support. For the first time on a Palma device, users can jot down quick notes, sign documents, mark up eBooks, and sketch ideas directly onto the black-and-white E Ink display. This shift turns what was once a dedicated reading gadget into an invaluable pocket-sized notepad.

Standout Features at a Glance

  • Precision Stylus Compatibility: Write and sketch effortlessly with low-latency stylus support designed for quick note-taking on the go.
  • Refined Ergonomic Design: A slimmer, modern chassis with an enhanced grip texture for comfortable one-handed reading sessions.
  • Faster Refresh Rates: Upgraded E Ink display technology delivers smoother page turns and reduced ghosting during daily navigation.
  • Expanded Android Ecosystem: Snappy performance allows seamless integration with popular reading apps, digital notebooks, and audiobooks.

Is the Price Bump Worth It?

When the Palma 2 debuted in 2024 with a $279.99 price tag, it offered an accessible entry point into distraction-free reading. The Palma 3 commands a noticeable price increase to match its expanded hardware capabilities, premium materials, and digitizer layer. Whether this price bump is justified depends heavily on your daily workflow. If you strictly want a simple pocket e-reader, older models might still suffice. But for power users seeking a sleek, distraction-free secondary device that seamlessly blends e-reading with digital note-taking, the Boox Palma 3 stands out as one of the most compelling portable tech releases of the year.
