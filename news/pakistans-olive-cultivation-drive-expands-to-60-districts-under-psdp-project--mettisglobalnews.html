Pakistan's olive cultivation drive expands to 60 districts under PSDP project - mettisglobal.news

Published by AI Pipeline | September 23, 2026 at 12:06 PM

ISLAMABAD — In a transformative leap toward agricultural innovation and economic self-reliance, Pakistan has officially expanded its national olive cultivation drive across 60 districts nationwide. Operating under the flagship Public Sector Development Programme (PSDP) project, "Promotion of Olive Cultivation on Commercial Scale," this aggressive initiative aims to turn vast tracts of marginal land into high-yielding orchards, positioning the country as an emerging player in the global olive oil market.

Scaling the 'Green Gold' Tech Revolution

Driven by climate-smart agricultural techniques and precision farming tech, the PSDP initiative targets regions with optimal microclimates, including Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, and Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Agricultural scientists and tech experts are leveraging geographic information systems (GIS) and soil mapping technology to identify ideal micro-zones. By introducing drought-tolerant, high-yielding European olive varieties, the project ensures maximum crop resilience against changing weather patterns while optimizing water usage through smart drip irrigation systems.

Slashing Imports & Empowering Local Farmers

Pakistan currently spends billions of dollars annually on importing edible oils, making olive farming a vital strategic pivot. The expansion to 60 districts is not merely an agricultural goal; it is an economic imperative designed to drastically reduce reliance on foreign imports. Beyond tree plantation, the federal and provincial governments are establishing modern olive processing facilities, state-of-the-art extraction plants, and certified testing laboratories across key hubs. These technological additions allow local farmers to press extra virgin olive oil locally, ensuring international quality standards and opening lucrative revenue streams.

Key Highlights of the PSDP Olive Expansion

  • Nationwide Footprint: Scaled up to cover 60 strategic districts across all major provinces and regions.
  • Tech Integration: Deployment of modern drip-irrigation networks, solar-powered pumps, and automated extraction facilities.
  • Economic Relief: Aimed at curbing the massive national edible oil import bill while boosting rural GDP.
  • Farmer Support: Provision of subsidized saplings, technical training, and direct access to processing centers.

As Pakistan scales up its olive infrastructure, the long-term vision extends toward building a recognized export brand—"Pak-Olive." With thousands of certified hectares already thriving and advanced agro-tech taking root, Pakistan’s olive revolution is proving to be a masterclass in modernizing traditional agriculture into a sustainable, high-tech economic powerhouse.
