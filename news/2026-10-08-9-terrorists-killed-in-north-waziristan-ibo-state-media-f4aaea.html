9 terrorists killed in North Waziristan IBO: state media

Published on October 08, 2026

According to Dawn, citing state media reports on Wednesday, security forces executed intelligence-based operations in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's North Waziristan district, resulting in the deaths of nine terrorists. The targeted raids occurred in areas including Palangzai, Nadeem Kot, and Kot Khazri.

State media reported that the deceased belonged to Fitna al-Khawarij, a term the state uses to refer to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan. Four of those killed were described as major commanders, though their individual identities were not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Troops also destroyed several hideouts and confiscated a significant quantity of arms and ammunition.

What happened

The anti-terror operations in North Waziristan successfully dismantled multiple militant positions. Following the action, state-run APP reported that President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif commended the security personnel for the operation's success. President Zardari praised the bravery of the forces and stated that local and foreign terror conspiracies would be defeated, while Prime Minister Sharif reiterated the nation's resolve to fully eliminate terrorism.

Key facts

  • Nine terrorists were killed, four of whom were key commanders.
  • The actions took place in Palangzai, Nadeem Kot, and Kot Khazri.
  • Multiple hideouts were destroyed, and weapons and ammunition were recovered.
  • President Zardari and Prime Minister Sharif issued official statements praising the military's actions.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has experienced ongoing security instability. Figures from the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies show that non-merged districts in the province saw 54 terrorist attacks in August, a 50 percent increase from the 36 attacks recorded in July.

This recent raid is part of a series of military engagements in the area. A day prior, security forces killed 13 terrorists in North Waziristan, with additional casualties inflicted on militants in late September and early October. The operations follow severe militant violence, including a September 26 suicide blast in Dera Ismail Khan that killed 12 people and a September 18 attack in Kohat that left 23 dead.
