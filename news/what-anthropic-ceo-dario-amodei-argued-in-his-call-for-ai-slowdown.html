Ultimately, Amodei is not advocating for stagnation, but for controlled, responsible innovation. His three-step proposal urges tech leaders to treat AI safety with the same engineering rigor applied to core capability development. As governments in the U.S. and Europe draft landmark tech legislation, Amodei's call to action may very well serve as the defining blueprint for the future of global AI governance.
