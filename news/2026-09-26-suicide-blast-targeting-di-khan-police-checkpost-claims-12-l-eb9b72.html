Suicide blast targeting DI Khan police checkpost claims 12 lives, injures 30

Published by AI Pipeline | September 26, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, a suicide bomber drove an explosives-laden tractor trolley into the Aman Mela police checkpost in the Darazinda tehsil of Dera Ismail Khan on Saturday. The explosion destroyed most of the checkpost and completely demolished an adjacent home. Local residents heard the blast over a considerable distance, followed by heavy gunfire in the area.

Shortly after the initial blast, a Rescue 1122 ambulance carrying bodies of victims from the scene came under direct gunfire. This separate shooting resulted in the death of one rescue worker and left two others seriously injured.

Key facts

  • The suicide attack resulted in 12 fatalities, including nine men, two women, a minor, and a Customs official. A total of 35 people were injured, while police confirmed no security personnel died in the blast.
  • The targeted checkpost is located near Mughal Kot along the DI Khan-Quetta Road, close to the border between Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan.
  • In the ambulance attack, rescue worker Shareefullah was killed, while personnel named Anees and Imtiaz suffered severe injuries.
  • More than 25 personnel, 10 ambulances, and a fire truck assisted in relief operations, despite challenges caused by unavailable mobile phone coverage in the area.
  • Key state officials, including President Asif Ali Zardari, Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi, and PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari, issued statements condemning the attack and directing full medical care for the wounded.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has experienced a rising wave of terrorist activity aimed at security forces and law enforcement, leading authorities to step up counterterrorism efforts. The Aman Mela post serves as an important site for monitoring traffic and preventing smuggling along the provincial border. Information regarding a direct claim of responsibility by a specific group was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
