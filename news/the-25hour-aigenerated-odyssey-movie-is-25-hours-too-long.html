Watching Homer's hero navigate the Aegean Sea for 150 minutes feels less like an epic adventure and more like an endless technical glitch.

While AI technology continues to make valuable strides in visual effects pipelines and pre-production conceptualization, Odysseus: The Fall stands as a definitive caution tag for the industry.
