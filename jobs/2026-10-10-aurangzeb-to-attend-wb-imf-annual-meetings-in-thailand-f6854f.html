Aurangzeb to attend WB-IMF annual meetings in Thailand

Published on October 10, 2026

Role Overview

This update details the participation of Pakistan's Federal Minister for Finance and Revenue, Senator Muhammad Aurangzeb, at the World Bank Group-IMF Annual Meetings 2026 in Bangkok, Thailand. Leading Pakistan's delegation, the finance minister is undertaking an extensive schedule featuring over 60 high-level engagements with global policymakers, financial institutions, central bank governors, development partners, and credit rating agencies. The visit focuses on advancing structural economic reforms, private sector development, and Pakistan's transition toward sustainable, export-led growth.

Key Details

  • Event: World Bank Group-IMF Annual Meetings 2026
  • Location: Bangkok, Thailand
  • Event Dates: 12th to 18th of this month
  • Head of Delegation: Senator Muhammad Aurangzeb (Federal Minister for Finance and Revenue)
  • Primary Objectives: Demonstrating Pakistan's macroeconomic stability, advancing structural reforms, promoting financial inclusion, job creation, and expanding engagement with global capital markets
  • Key Engagements: Discussions with leadership from the IMF, World Bank Group, IFC, MIGA, AIIB, OPEC Fund, Saudi Fund for Development, ITFC, JICA, ECO Trade and Development Bank, UN ESCAP, IOM, US Department of the Treasury, and bilateral meetings with delegates from Saudi Arabia, China, Turkiye, Iran, the UK, and Thailand
  • Financial Institutions & Rating Agencies: Engagements with Fitch Ratings, Moody's, S&P Global, JP Morgan, Citi, Deutsche Bank, Standard Chartered, Barclays, Bank of America, Jefferies International, MSCI, and Alibaba International Digital Commerce
