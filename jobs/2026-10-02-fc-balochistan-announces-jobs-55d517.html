FC Balochistan Announces Jobs

Published by AI Pipeline | October 02, 2026

Role Overview

Frontier Corps (FC) Balochistan North has announced recruitment under Recruit Course 71 for candidates across Pakistan. The available vacancies include General Duty (GD Sipahi), EME, Signal, and Combat Clerk positions. Appointments will be made according to recruitment rules and merit standards.

Eligibility Criteria

  • General Duty: Non-local candidates must have passed Matriculation, while local Baloch applicants require a Middle qualification.
  • EME Soldier: Candidates must hold an FSc Pre-Engineering qualification in the first division. Those with a Diploma of Associate Engineering (DAE) will be given preference.
  • Signal Soldier & Combat Clerk: Applicants must hold an FA or ICS qualification.
  • Age Limit: Non-local applicants must be between 18 and 23 years old. Local Baloch applicants can be between 18 and 26 years old. Age is calculated as of 20 September 2026.
  • Height Requirements: Non-local candidates require a minimum height of 5 feet 6 inches, while local Baloch candidates require 5 feet 3 inches.
  • Relaxations: Sons and brothers of martyrs receive a 2-year age relaxation and a 2-inch height relaxation.
  • Ineligibility & Verification: Police verification is mandatory. Applicants who use drugs, submit fake documents, or were dismissed from government jobs for misconduct are ineligible.

Key Details

  • Number of Posts: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Salary: Selected personnel will receive pay comparable to Pakistan Army standards (exact monthly amount: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice).
  • Benefits: Free meals and lodging, medical care for spouses and children, half-fare train travel during leave, performance promotions, UN mission opportunities, and free education and housing for martyrs' children.
  • Important Dates: Overall recruitment continues until 10 October 2026. A dedicated period for local Baloch and Sindhi applicants runs from 1 to 30 September 2026.
  • Recruitment Locations: Balochistan sites include FC headquarters in Mach, Quetta, Nushki, Chaman, Muslim Bagh, Zhob, Loralai, Sui, Dera Bugti, Sibi, Barkhan, and Kohlu. Sindh applicants must report to Headquarters Sui Rifles or Headquarters Sibi Scouts.

How to Apply

Applicants can register online via the official FC Balochistan North recruitment portal. After completing the online process, candidates must report to their assigned recruitment centre on their scheduled dates. Candidates must bring original educational certificates, domicile documents, CNIC (a B-Form is not accepted for applicants aged 18 or older), required photocopies, four white-background passport photos, and relevant verification records or pension books for martyr concessions.
