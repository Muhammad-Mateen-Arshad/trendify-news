Wounded US troops say Iran drone threat was underestimated - AP

Published on October 06, 2026

Role Overview

This update references recent news concerning US military operations stationed in Kuwait. According to reports gathered by the Associated Press from 13 service members, leadership was notified that an operations center at Port Shuaiba lacked sufficient drone protection. Despite these warnings, personnel remained at the location during Operation Epic Fury against Iran. A strike on March 1 subsequently caused casualties among American soldiers. The military completed an inquiry into the event in June, but the findings have not been made public, and official spokespersons have declined to elaborate on the accounts.
