Why is property ownership a condition for government jobs?

Published on October 08, 2026

Navigating public sector job opportunities requires understanding both evaluation procedures and administrative verification requirements. In public recruitment processes, such as those for primary school assistant teacher and police constable positions, candidates undergo background checks to confirm their local residency.

Role Overview

Candidates selected for government posts, including primary school assistant teachers and police constables, complete competitive selection stages including written examinations and viva tests. Field-level verification is then conducted to confirm candidate identities and maintain the integrity of district-based recruitment systems.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualifications and residency standards noted in official selection frameworks include:

  • Academic Requirements: Assistant teacher posts have selected candidates holding graduation and post-graduation qualifications, such as degrees from Islamic University. For other roles, educational criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Residency Status: Under the Birth and Death Registration Rules, 2018, a permanent address is established by residing at a location for at least three years. Land or home ownership is not legally required, protecting candidates who live in rented accommodation or were displaced by river erosion or poverty.
  • Constitutional Protections: Articles 27 and 29 guarantee equality before the law and equal employment opportunities in public service without discrimination based on place of birth.

Key Details

  • Verification Focus: Official background checks assess genuine residential history (such as living at an address over recent years) rather than property ownership status.
  • Post Locations: District-based positions exist across various regions, including Jhenaidah, Barishal, Bhola, and Dhaka.
  • Vacancies, Salaries, and Deadlines: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
