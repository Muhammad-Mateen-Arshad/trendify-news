Paramount wraps up $110 billion Warner Bros Discovery deal

Published on October 09, 2026

Major structural shifts are unfolding across the global media sector following the official completion of Paramount Skydance's $110 billion takeover of Warner Bros. Discovery. The merged business now operates under the corporate banner Skydance, bringing major television networks, streaming platforms, and film studios under unified leadership.

Role Overview

The executive structure places Chief Executive Officer David Ellison in charge of overarching strategy and creative vision. Joining him in top management is former Mattel chief executive Ynon Kreiz, who acts as co-CEO handling daily operations and organizational integration. In news leadership, Mark Thompson remains head of CNN, while Bari Weiss continues as Editor-in-Chief at CBS News. Regulatory filings show that Ellison receives a $5 million annual base salary with an additional $5 million target bonus.

Workforce Impact and Key Details

The consolidation creates a unified entertainment enterprise, though workforce adjustments are anticipated across the broader media industry as the business pursues financial efficiency. Operational targets and transaction facts include:

  • Transaction Value: US$110 billion acquisition completed on October 6.
  • Efficiency Target: $6 billion in planned cost savings, with significant portions aimed at combining cloud providers and streaming technologies.
  • Industry Employment Impact: Despite a focus on non-labor savings, the total scale of reductions is expected to affect jobs throughout Hollywood.
  • Product Plans: Strategies include consolidating services like HBO Max and Paramount+, alongside commitments to release at least 30 films annually during the first two years.
  • Trading Ticker: Shares trade under ticker symbol "SKYD" on the New York Stock Exchange.
