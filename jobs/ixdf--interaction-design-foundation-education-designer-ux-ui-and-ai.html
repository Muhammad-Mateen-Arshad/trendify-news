Are you a talented designer with a passion for teaching? Have you facilitated workshops, created online courses, or mentored designers and realized you are unusually great at it? Role Overview

In this high-impact remote position, you will transition from teaching hundreds to educating hundreds of thousands. You’ll be responsible for planning, structuring, and creating core course material, practical exercises, and assessments across UX, UI, human-centered design, and AI. Best of all, you will co-create educational experiences alongside design legends like Don Norman and Vitaly Friedman, as well as Design Leads from Netflix, Meta, LinkedIn, and Adobe.

Why You'll Love This Role

  • Unmatched Global Impact: Your educational design decisions will directly empower learners across 100+ countries to build better careers.

  • Remote Work + Travel Perks: Enjoy working from your home office, paired with exciting opportunities to fly out a few times a year to shoot courses in professional studios globally.
  • AI Innovation: Pioneer how design and AI are taught, helping designers master modern AI tools without losing timeless human-centered principles.

Key Requirements

  • At least 5 years of hands-on design experience (UX, UI, HCI, or AI).
  • Proven experience running workshops, mentoring, or creating educational curricula.
  • A built project you can show (a course, workshop series, teaching app, or live Master Class).
  • Mastery in turning complex topics into clear, engaging, step-by-step learning paths.
  • Enthusiastic about using AI natively to research, structure, and build content faster.
  • Located in time zones between UTC-6 and UTC+8 (includes Europe, the Americas, Africa, and parts of Asia).
