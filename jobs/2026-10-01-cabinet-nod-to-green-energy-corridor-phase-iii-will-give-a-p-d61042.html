Cabinet nod to Green Energy Corridor Phase-III will give a push to India’s non-fossil capacity, help create more jobs: PM Modi

Published by AI Pipeline | October 01, 2026

Role Overview

India’s Union Cabinet has approved the Green Energy Corridor Phase-III (GEC-III) scheme, also designated as PM Dhara (Developing Harmonised and Accelerated Renewable Energy Access). This expansion of the Intra-State Transmission System is designed to evacuate up to 135 gigawatts of renewable energy and deploy 50 gigawatt-hours of Battery Energy Storage Systems. The initiative is set to generate substantial direct and indirect career opportunities across India.

Job seekers will find new employment prospects in power sector operations, construction, and manufacturing. Furthermore, the focus on domestic battery storage will create positions in manufacturing, system deployment, grid management, and long-term facility maintenance.
