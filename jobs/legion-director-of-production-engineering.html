Legion: Director of Production Engineering

Are you a visionary engineering leader passionate about building reliable, automated, and hyper-secure cloud platforms? Legion Technologies is hiring a remote Director of Production Engineering to lead, scale, and fortify their modern AWS infrastructure. If you thrive in high-impact roles combining strategic team leadership with hands-on technical architecture, this opportunity is for you!

Role Overview

Legion is on a mission to revolutionize hourly work using AI-driven workforce management. In this high-visibility position, you'll spend ~70-80% of your time mentoring engineering teams and driving roadmaps, and ~20-30% staying hands-on with system architecture, tooling, and incident response. You will take full ownership of Legion's AWS cloud ecosystem, security operations (SecOps), and modern SRE practices.

Compensation: $220,000 – $265,000 base + performance bonus + stock equity.

  • Build & Lead: Hire, mentor, and empower a world-class, globally distributed DevOps and SRE team.

  • Champion SecOps & Observability: Oversee threat detection, vulnerability management, and automated alerting using platforms like Datadog.
  • Pioneer AI Integration: Apply cutting-edge agentic AI workflows to SDLC and DevOps pipelines for automated investigation and remediation.
  • Drive Engineering Velocity: Advance Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform), CI/CD practices, and automated testing to eliminate operational toil.

What You Need to Succeed

  • 8–12+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or production engineering, including active people management.
  • Deep expertise operating enterprise AWS environments with Kubernetes (EKS) and RDS at scale.
  • Strong background running SecOps, incident response, and Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform/CloudFormation).
  • Hands-on coding skills in Go, Python, or Bash on Linux/Unix platforms.

Why You'll Love Working Here

  • 100% remote flexibility anywhere in the United States.
  • $0 monthly medical, dental, and vision premiums starting on day one.
  • Discretionary PTO, 401(k), equity, monthly wellness stipends, and "Lunch on Legion" perks.
