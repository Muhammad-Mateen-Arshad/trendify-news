Defending Pakistan’s auto base

Published on October 05, 2026

Role Overview

Pakistan’s automotive component manufacturing sector offers extensive opportunities for skilled technical personnel across major industrial hubs. Key production clusters are established in Port Qasim, Hub, Karachi's SITE and Korangi industrial estates, and the Lahore–Sheikhupura corridor. Factories in these areas manufacture a wide array of vehicle parts, including windshields, seats, fuel tanks, wiring, lights, exhaust systems, sheet-metal assemblies, rubber products, moulded components, and specialized engine sub-assemblies.

According to industry estimates, the sector employs approximately 300,000 people. These technical roles support major long-term vehicle assemblers like Suzuki, Toyota, and Honda, as well as new international entrants entering the market.
