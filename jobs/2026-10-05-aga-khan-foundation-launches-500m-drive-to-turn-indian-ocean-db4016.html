Aga Khan Foundation launches $500M drive to turn Indian Ocean restoration into economic opportunity

Published on October 05, 2026

The Aga Khan Foundation has announced a major ten-year program titled ReGeneration. This $500 million effort aims to restore coastal ecosystems and build economic opportunities across six nations in the Indian Ocean area. The initiative links nature conservation directly with local financial growth by supporting eco-friendly livelihoods, enterprise development, clean energy, regenerative agriculture, and sustainable fishing.

Role Overview

Through ReGeneration, the Aga Khan Foundation plans to rehabilitate 150,000 hectares of shorelines and mangroves while generating new income streams and employment. Rather than treating environmental protection and financial development as competing priorities, the program helps local communities build sustainable businesses. Focus sectors include clean energy expansion, regenerative farming practices such as the AgroVida project in Cabo Delgado, and sustainable fisheries across coastal regions.
