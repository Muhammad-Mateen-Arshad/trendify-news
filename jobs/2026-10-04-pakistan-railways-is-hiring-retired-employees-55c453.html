Pakistan Railways is Hiring Retired Employees

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

Pakistan Railways has announced job opportunities for retired supervisory staff seeking contract-based re-employment. The vacancies are available within the Carriage & Wagon Department and the Mechanical Loco Department. Successful applicants will be appointed for an initial contract period of one year, which may be extended for an additional year if required. Selected candidates can be posted anywhere across the Pakistan Railways network.

Role Overview

This recruitment drive aims to bring eligible retired employees back into service across technical and supervisory functions. The available pay scales for these positions include BS-06, BS-09, BS-11, BS-12, and BS-14, depending on the post assigned.

Key Details

  • Carriage & Wagon Department Vacancies: 21 posts
  • Loco Department Vacancies: 24 posts across various technical categories
  • Salary & Benefits: Remuneration will be paid according to the candidate's last pay drawn, along with standard allowances.
  • Job Duration: Initial 1-year contract, extendable for 1 further year.
  • Application Fee: Rs. 500 postal order (mandatory).
  • Application Deadline: October 15, 2026.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant Status: Only retired employees who previously served in the advertised categories are eligible to apply.
  • Specific Qualifications: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice for individual post criteria. Candidates must meet the required experience and qualification standards for their relevant category.
  • Age Limit: Maximum age limit is 62 years.
  • Ineligibility: Former employees who were compulsorily removed, dismissed, or retired on medical grounds cannot apply.
  • Medical Fitness: Candidates must clear a physical medical fitness examination at a Pakistan Railways hospital. No TA/DA will be provided for interviews or medical tests.

How to Apply

Interested retired personnel must submit a typed application form containing complete personal and professional details. Applications must include a Rs. 500 postal order as a processing fee, along with attested copies of the following documents:

  • Educational certificates
  • CNIC
  • Railway Identity Card or Experience Certificate
  • PPO, LPC, and Service Certificate
  • Three recent passport-size photographs
  • Three postal envelopes stamped with the applicant's complete address and RAD card details

Completed applications must reach the Chief Personnel Officer, Pakistan Railways, Headquarters Office, Empress Road, Lahore, by October 15, 2026. Submissions received after the closing date or without the processing fee will not be accepted.
