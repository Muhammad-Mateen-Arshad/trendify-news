HEC Announces Jobs With 6-Figure Salaries

Published by AI Pipeline | October 01, 2026

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) has invited applications from Pakistani nationals for nine contract positions under development projects funded by the Public Sector Development Program (PSDP). Selected candidates may be posted anywhere in Pakistan, with monthly compensation ranging from Rs. 40,700 to Rs. 398,130 depending on the job.

Role Overview

The advertised openings include positions for Project Director, Law Officer, Project Manager, Project Accountant, Office Assistant, and Attendant across different development projects, such as PhD scholarship programs and university development initiatives.

Eligibility Criteria

Requirements vary depending on the chosen role:

  • Project Director: Requires a 16-year master's degree (minimum second division) from an HEC-recognized university and seven years of experience, including five years in managerial roles after earning the final degree.
  • Law Officer: Requires an LLB or equivalent degree from an HEC-recognized institution, five years of experience as a practicing lawyer in corporate or services law, strong communication skills, and MS Office proficiency.
  • Project Manager: Requires a 16-year master's or bachelor's degree (minimum second division) from an HEC-recognized institution, five years of relevant experience (including required supervisory or middle-management experience), and skills in MS Office and MS Project.
  • Project Accountant: Requires an MBA in Finance, MCom, or CMA (16 years of education, second division) with three years of experience, including one year in middle management post-qualification.
  • Office Assistant: Requires a 14-year graduation degree (second division) from an HEC-recognized institution, two years of work experience, and advanced MS Office skills.
  • Attendant: Requires a Matric qualification and one year of relevant experience.

Experience is calculated strictly after acquiring the minimum educational qualification; internships during studies, apprenticeships, part-time jobs, and honorary service are excluded. Exact numerical age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice, though advertised limits include a general five-year relaxation.

Key Details

  • Vacancies: 9 contract posts
  • Salary Range: Rs. 40,700 to Rs. 398,130 per month
  • Posting Location: Anywhere in Pakistan
  • Application Fee: Rs. 1,000
  • Test Locations: Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, and Quetta
  • Deadline: 14 October 2026

How to Apply

Interested candidates must apply online through the HEC careers portal by 14 October 2026. Applicants currently in government service must apply through the proper channel. The Rs. 1,000 application fee can be paid via 1Link using a mobile wallet or banking app. Printed copies of documents or CVs are not required. Shortlisting will be based on online profiles, and HEC will not offer travel or daily allowances for tests or interviews.
