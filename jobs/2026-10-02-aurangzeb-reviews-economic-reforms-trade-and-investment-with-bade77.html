Role Overview

Federal Minister for Finance and Revenue, Senator Muhammad Aurangzeb, recently conferred with a World Bank delegation headed by Country Director Bolormaa Amgaabazar to review Pakistan's economic reform partnership. The talks emphasized shifting from planning to implementing practical measures that foster employment, enhance the business climate, and spur growth. Key discussions centered on a proposed growth and jobs operation designed to ease business constraints, expand access to finance for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), and introduce targeted sectoral reforms. Furthermore, the meeting addressed labour-market alignment and skills development, including updates to the national vocational qualifications framework, broader international recognition of local skills, and the creation of formal overseas migration pathways backed by digital platforms.
