Legion: Chief Architect

Published by AI Pipeline | September 19, 2026

Ready to lead the technical vision for an AI-native platform that is transforming work for over 75 million hourly employees? Legion Technologies is looking for a hands-on, visionary Chief Architect to serve as their senior-most technical authority and shape the architectural foundation powering the next decade of innovation.

Role Overview

This is not a strategy-only advisory role—it is a high-impact, hands-on opportunity to live close to the code, challenge assumptions, and solve the hardest technical problems in distributed systems, AI, and enterprise software. Operating 100% remotely in the United States, you will partner directly with engineering leaders and executive stakeholders to turn bold product ambitions into resilient, enterprise-grade production systems.

Key Responsibilities

  • Serve as the top technical authority for Legion’s Workforce Management platform, owning architectural choices across AI, optimization engines, distributed systems, and real-time decision platforms.
  • Write code prototypes, reference architectures, and design specs to unblock teams and set clear technical direction.
  • Architect production AI capabilities, including LLM workflows, feature platforms, retrieval, and continuous feedback loops.
  • Drive system scalability, performance, and security (SOC 2, ISO 27001) for large enterprise customers.
  • Mentor Staff and Principal Engineers, fostering a culture of technical rigor, pragmatic execution, and continuous learning.

Requirements

  • 15+ years of experience designing and scaling mission-critical, multi-tenant SaaS platforms and distributed systems.
  • Proven track record as a Chief Architect, Distinguished Engineer, or Senior Principal Engineer.
  • Deep expertise with modern cloud environments (AWS, GCP, Azure) and backend languages like Java, Kotlin, Go, Python, or Scala.
  • Hands-on experience architecting AI, machine learning, optimization solvers, or data-intensive engines in production.

Why Join & Perks

In addition to shaping the future of work, Legion offers an outstanding compensation package featuring a base salary of $300,000 – $385,000 + Bonus + Equity. Perks include $0 monthly premium healthcare, 401(k), discretionary PTO, paid parental leave, monthly wellness reimbursements, and "Lunch on Legion"—all delivered in a flexible, remote-first environment.
