PPSC Advertisement No. 11 2026 Announced

Published on October 09, 2026

Role Overview

The Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) has published Advertisement No. 11/2026, offering 299 government jobs across multiple departments in Punjab. This recruitment drive covers a wide range of roles including doctors, engineers, intelligence officers, veterinary professionals, sports managers, and finance staff across departments such as Punjab Police, Agriculture, Housing, Youth Affairs, and Livestock.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualifications depend on the specific position being applied for:

  • Punjab Police Special Branch: 30 Intelligence Officer (BS-14) posts require a second-division graduation degree with basic computer literacy, or a graduation degree plus five years of experience in a law-enforcement agency's intelligence wing.
  • Specialized Healthcare: Over 100 Senior Registrar positions require an MBBS or equivalent medical degree, postgraduate specialization (such as FCPS, MS, FRCS, MRCP, MD, or American Board Diplomat), and required professional registration.
  • Livestock and Dairy Development: Offers 61 BS-18 Senior Veterinary Officer positions and 40 BS-17 Veterinary Officer posts. Applicants need a DVM, MPhil, PhD, or relevant degree along with valid Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council (PVMC) registration.
  • Youth Affairs and Sports: 37 BS-16 roles require a second-division Master’s degree in Physical Education or Sports Sciences, or a second-division Bachelor’s degree for international-level Pakistani athletes.
  • Housing and Town Planning (PHATA): 16 vacancies, including Deputy Directors (requiring relevant Civil Engineering or City/Regional Planning degrees, PEC/PCATP registration, and experience) and 12 Assistant Accounts Officers (accepting BCom, MCom, ACCA, MBA, or BS Accounting and Finance).
  • Agriculture Department: 8 Assistant Regional Manager (BS-17) posts require a BSc in Agricultural, Civil, or Mechanical Engineering with PEC registration.
  • Age Limit & Domicile: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Advertisement Date: October 9, 2026
  • Application Deadline: October 26, 2026
  • Total Vacancies: 299 posts
  • Test Format: The Intelligence Officer exam is a 90-minute, 100-mark test covering general ability (80%) and basic computer studies (20%). Test details for other roles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must submit their applications online through the official PPSC portal. Required fee payments can be made via ATMs, internet banking, mobile banking, 1Link member branches, JazzCash, Easypaisa, UPaisa, or other supported channels. Government employees should apply through the proper channel and secure an NOC or DPC where applicable. Candidates claiming equivalent academic degrees must provide an equivalence certificate issued by the relevant authority.
