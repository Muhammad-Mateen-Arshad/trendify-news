New IT Hub Opens in Zhob to Boost Digital Skills and Jobs

Published on October 07, 2026

A new information technology center has officially opened in the Zhob district of Balochistan. Launched under the Balochistan Special Development Initiative, the facility was established with support from the Balochistan provincial government, the Pakistan Army, and Frontier Corps Balochistan. The project aims to enhance digital literacy, build technical capabilities, and expand employment prospects for local youth in remote areas.

Role Overview

The facility provides a local platform for young residents to prepare for opportunities in the digital economy. Zhob's geographical distance from major urban centers like Quetta has traditionally limited access to specialized professional training. This initiative brings technology closer to home, offering access to fields such as software development, freelancing, digital services, and online work.

Key Details

  • Location: Zhob district, Balochistan
  • Initiative: Balochistan Special Development Initiative
  • Supporting Bodies: Pakistan Army, Frontier Corps Balochistan, and the provincial government
  • Focus Areas: Digital literacy, technical training, software development, freelancing, and online work
