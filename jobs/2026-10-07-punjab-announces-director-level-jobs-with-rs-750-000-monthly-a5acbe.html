Punjab Announces Director-Level Jobs With Rs. 750,000 Monthly Salary

Published on October 07, 2026

The Punjab Education Initiatives Management Authority (PEIMA), an entity under the School Education Department, Government of Punjab, has announced recruitments for six contract-based positions. The openings include high-level directorships offering monthly packages up to Rs. 750,000, as well as roles in audit and training.

Role Overview

PEIMA is filling one vacancy for each of the six advertised contract positions. These postings cover human resources and administration, partnerships and collaboration, academics and research, monitoring and evaluation, internal audit, and training operations within the authority.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Director Positions (HR & Admin, Partnerships, Academics, Monitoring & Evaluation): Candidates require at least 16 years of education in a relevant discipline from an HEC-recognised university, along with 10 years of post-qualification experience (including 3 years at a senior level). Applicants holding MPhil or PhD degrees will be preferred.
  • Chief Audit Officer: Applicants must hold ACCA, ICMA, or CA credentials, or possess 16 years of education in Finance, Accounting, Commerce, or an equivalent discipline. A minimum of 5 years of relevant post-qualification experience in internal audit or financial controls is required.
  • Assistant Director (Training & OAT): Candidates must have 16 years of education in Education Planning, HRM, Business Administration, Social Sciences, or a related field, combined with at least 3 years of post-qualification experience. Preference will be given to those with experience in government agencies or donor organisations.

Key Details

  • Total Positions: 6 vacancies (1 post per role)
  • Monthly Salary: Up to Rs. 750,000 for Director roles, Rs. 350,000 for Chief Audit Officer, and Rs. 225,000 for Assistant Director
  • Job Location: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Processing Fee: Non-refundable Rs. 3,000 for each application
  • Deadline: October 26, 2026

How to Apply

Eligible candidates must submit their applications online via the Punjab Job Portal on or before October 26, 2026. Submissions made by hand, courier, or email will be rejected. Applicants must pay the non-refundable processing fee of Rs. 3,000 for each chosen post and upload payment proof with their online submission. Candidates working in government or public-sector organisations must apply through proper channels with a valid No Objection Certificate (NOC). Only shortlisted candidates will be invited for written testing or interviews, and no TA/DA will be provided.
