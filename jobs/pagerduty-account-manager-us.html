  • Account Retention & Growth: Own a dedicated portfolio of existing accounts to ensure maximum value realization and churn mitigation.

  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with Sales Operations, Legal, and Renewals teams to execute seamless contracting and accurate forecasting.

What You Need to Succeed

  • Hands-on expertise with Salesforce (SFDC) and analytics platforms like Gainsight, Looker, or Tableau.
  • Experience with enterprise frameworks like MEDDPICC, Value Selling, or DevOps environments is highly preferred.
