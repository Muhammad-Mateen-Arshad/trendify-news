Pakistani Entrepreneur Builds AI-Powered Search Tool for All Govt Services

Published by AI Pipeline | October 02, 2026

Pakistani job seekers and citizens looking for reliable public service information can now utilize Ask Pakistan, a new independent AI-driven search platform created by developer Raheel Siddiqui. Inspired by the United States platform America.gov, this tool gathers official information across public sector websites into a single conversational interface.

Role Overview

Ask Pakistan allows users to search for details regarding government jobs, CNICs, passports, taxes, education, and social support. The platform indexes content from key official entities, including the Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC), NADRA, FBR, BISP, HEC, PTA, police departments, utility providers, courts, and provincial governments. However, specific employment positions or job vacancies are not detailed in the source.

Key Details

  • Search Capabilities: Questions can be asked in English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu. Voice inputs are also supported, with recordings transcribed via Google's Gemini speech service without being stored by the platform.
  • Official Sources: The service searches over 5,000 indexed pages and features a public directory of 212 federal, provincial, and regulatory websites. Answers include direct source links and suggested next steps.
  • Privacy Measures: Personal data—including CNIC, passport, phone, email, and bank numbers—is removed in the user's browser before queries are submitted. Conversation histories are not saved or linked to individuals.
  • Service Boundaries: Ask Pakistan is an independent tool and is not an official government entity. It cannot complete transactions, submit application forms, or access private government files.
