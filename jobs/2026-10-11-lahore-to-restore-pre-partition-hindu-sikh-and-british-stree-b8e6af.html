Lahore To Restore Pre-Partition Hindu, Sikh And British Street Names

Published on October 11, 2026

The Punjab Cabinet in Pakistan, led by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz, has sanctioned a proposal to restore historic pre-Partition names for various roads, public sites, and neighborhoods throughout Lahore. This decision focuses on preserving the city's colonial, Hindu, and Sikh historical identity. However, specific recruitment information or job opportunities associated with this project have not been announced.

Key Details

The main points of the approved government initiative include:

  • Reinstating historic names for sites and areas such as Laxmi Chowk, Krishan Nagar, Dharampura, Mohan Lal Bazaar, Shanti Nagar, and Bhagwan Pura.
  • Restoring original names for major thoroughfares, including Sant Nagar Jain Mandir Road, Queen’s Road, Empress Road, and Lawrence Road.
  • A plan suggested by former Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif to bring back historic cultural and sports areas inside Greater Iqbal Park, formerly known as Minto Park.
  • Refurbishing three traditional cricket grounds where Indian cricket figure Lala Amarnath trained, as well as acknowledging Minto Park's pre-1947 role as a venue for Dussehra events.
