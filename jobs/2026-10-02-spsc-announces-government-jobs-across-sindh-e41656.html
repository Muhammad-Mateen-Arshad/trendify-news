SPSC Announces Government Jobs Across Sindh

Published by AI Pipeline | October 02, 2026

Role Overview

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC) has issued Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, offering 54 government positions in Sindh. These opportunities span BPS-16 to BPS-18 scales across several key departments, including Home, Information, Law, Planning and Development, and General Administration.

Key Details

  • Total Openings: 54 vacancies
  • Pay Scales: BPS-16 through BPS-18
  • Application Deadline: October 23, 2026
  • Processing Fee: Rs. 500 (non-refundable)
  • Location: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Salary Package: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualifications depend on the post and department applied for:

  • Services, General Administration & Coordination Department: Deputy Director (I.T.) requires a bachelor's degree in IT/CS (at least second division or equivalent CGPA) plus five years of relevant IT project management experience. The Assistant Director (I.T.) role requires a similar bachelor's degree in IT or CS.
  • Home Department: Medical Officer (32 male posts) and Lady Medical Officer (4 posts) positions require an MBBS degree from a recognized institution, registration with the medical authority, and a completed one-year house job.
  • Information Department: Information Officer (4 posts) requires a master's degree in Mass Communication or Journalism alongside required experience.
  • Planning & Development Department: Assistant Director requires a 16-year or postgraduate qualification in Public Administration, Economics, HRM, or Public Policy. Program Officer requires 16 years of education in Software Engineering, IT, Computer Science, or related fields.
  • Law, Parliamentary Affairs & Criminal Prosecution Department: Assistant (10 posts) requires a graduation degree with at least a second division from an HEC-recognized university.
  • Age Requirements: Age limits differ by post, with standard government age relaxations applicable.

How to Apply

Applications must be submitted online through the SPSC portal, as physical applications are strictly not accepted. Candidates must generate a PSID code on the portal, pay the required Rs. 500 fee using available digital channels, and complete the submission process before October 23, 2026. Applicants should verify they fulfill all age, domicile, and educational requirements before finalizing their form.
