The Discovery

Beijing is home to the Liangma River, a historic waterway that was previously plagued by severe water quality issues. For a long period, the location was recognized as a neglected and foul-smelling backwater.

Why It Matters

The rejuvenation of this historic location has notably enhanced the standard of living in the Chinese capital. Today, the river serves as a thriving public space and stands as a major symbol of Beijing's wider urban environmental initiatives and green progress.
