Educational scholarships exist as part of the historical "Blood Alliance" between South Korea and Colombia, which was formed when Colombia deployed military forces during the Korean War.

Scholarship Overview

These educational opportunities serve to institutionalize and preserve the memory of the historical ties and sacrifices made during the military partnership between the two nations. Beyond diplomatic and economic ties, these scholarships represent an ongoing moral commitment between South Korea and Colombia.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility for this opportunity includes:

  • Descendants of veterans who served in the military deployment.
