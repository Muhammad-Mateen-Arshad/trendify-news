Role Overview

The Haryana Government has set up a Foreign Service Department designed to assist youth in finding employment overseas. This department sources vacancies from foreign countries, conducts selection processes, and assists candidates in securing jobs abroad. The initiative emphasizes transparent hiring practices for young job seekers.
