Finding official job updates and government service information in Pakistan often requires searching across multiple separate websites. A newly launched artificial intelligence search tool called Ask Pakistan aims to streamline this process by gathering information from hundreds of official government sources into a single conversational portal.

Role Overview

Developed by tech professional Raheel Siddiqui, the Ask Pakistan platform (PakistanGov.online) operates as an AI-powered search tool. It allows users to ask questions regarding government employment, passport services, tax details, and official documentation using English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu.

  • Platform Purpose: Centralized AI search for Pakistani government information, including job listings.
  • Connected Agencies: Covers information from 212 federal, provincial, and regulatory portals, including the Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC), Higher Education Commission (HEC), NADRA, FBR, BISP, and PTA.
  • Language Options: Supports questions asked in English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu.

Key Details

The platform provides direct information drawn from official sources and offers procedural guidance. Important technical and operational details include:

  • Indexed Content: The system has indexed over 5,000 pages spanning federal departments, provincial bodies, court systems, police departments, and utility providers.
  • Privacy Measures: Personal details such as CNIC numbers, passport details, phone numbers, and bank information are removed inside the browser before the query is sent. Conversation histories are not retained or linked to users.
  • Service Limitations: Ask Pakistan is not an official government entity. It cannot process applications, access confidential records, or conduct transactions on behalf of users.
