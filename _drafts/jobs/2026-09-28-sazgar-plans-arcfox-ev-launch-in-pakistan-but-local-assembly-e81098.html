Sazgar Engineering Works Ltd (SEWL) has announced major expansion plans in Pakistan's automotive sector, including the intended launch of ARCFOX new energy vehicles and the enhancement of its local operations. The company is carrying out a Rs22bn expansion programme that includes establishing a new paint shop, expected to finish within 9 to 10 months. Financing for this project relies on Rs17bn in debt, with the remaining portion covered by equity. SEWL is also preparing to launch the Canon Alpha in the coming months and recently exhibited models such as the Tank 300 and Jolion Max.
