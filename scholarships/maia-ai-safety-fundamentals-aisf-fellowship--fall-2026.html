Program Overview & Benefits

The AISF Fellowship is an intensive, 8-week reading group designed to guide participants through core concepts in technical AI safety, alignment research, and governance. By joining this fellowship, you will:

  • Engage with curated, high-level literature covering frontier topics in AI risk, interpretability, and technical alignment.
  • Participate in collaborative, small-group weekly discussions led by knowledgeable facilitators.
  • Connect directly with the vibrant MIT AI Alignment community, expanding your global academic and professional network.
  • Gain key insights that can kickstart a meaningful research career or policy focus dedicated to safe AI development.

Eligibility Criteria

This fellowship welcomes motivated students, researchers, developers, and policy enthusiasts from diverse backgrounds. To qualify, applicants should demonstrate:

  • A deep curiosity about AI safety, machine learning, or technical alignment challenges.
  • A strong commitment to complete required weekly readings and actively participate in all 8 sessions.
  • Analytical reasoning skills and the ability to engage thoughtfully with complex, technical, or philosophical concepts.
  • While technical backgrounds (such as computer science or mathematics) are advantageous, passionate individuals from policy, ethics, and other fields are equally encouraged to apply.

How to Apply

Taking your first step toward building a safer AI future is simple and straightforward:

  • Prepare and submit your application online well before the deadline on September 23, 2026.
  • Articulate your background, relevant skills, and specific interest in AI alignment within your application answers.
  • Emphasize how participating in the MIT AI Alignment ecosystem fits into your future academic or professional aspirations.
