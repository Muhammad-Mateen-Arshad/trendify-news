Step Up for Students scholarships help local homeschool family

Published by AI Pipeline | October 03, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Florida offers Step Up for Students scholarships to assist local families with educational choices for their children. Among the available state options is the Personalized Education Program (PEP) scholarship, which is tailored specifically for parents who choose homeschooling. Funding awards range from $500 to $7,800 depending on the scholarship. The PEP initiative currently serves 20,000 students and is expected to expand by 40,000 students annually.

Who Can Apply

Florida families can apply for these scholarship programs regardless of their household income level. The PEP track is open to families who opt for homeschooling rather than traditional public, charter, or private schooling. Specific academic criteria, application deadlines, and age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The PEP scholarship gives parents access to an education savings account that functions like a bank account. Key benefits of the program include:

  • Financial awards ranging from $500 to $7,800.
  • Coverage for curriculum costs, general school supplies, and setting up a dedicated home classroom.
  • The flexibility for parents to pull from different learning materials to challenge students according to their needs.
  • Direct reimbursements deposited onto a provided debit card, though processing reimbursement requests can take up to 60 days.

How to Apply

Parents can review program details and manage their funding through an online portal provided by Step Up for Students. Full application procedures, required documentation, and submission dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
