Erasmus Mundus MAPP Scholarship 2027–2029: Fully Funded Joint Master's in Public Policy - Global South Opportunities

Published by AI Pipeline | September 24, 2026

Welcome ambitious future leaders! The Erasmus Mundus MAPP (Master’s in Public Policy) Scholarship for 2027–2029 offers an extraordinary educational journey for talented international students, particularly those from Pakistan and across the Global South. This prestigious joint Master's programme allows you to study across top European universities, building an international network while gaining cutting-edge knowledge in global governance, public administration, and policy analysis.

This award is Fully Funded, ensuring that financial constraints do not hinder high-achieving scholars from pursuing world-class higher education in Europe.

What is Included

Recipients of the Erasmus Mundus MAPP scholarship receive comprehensive financial support, including:

  • Full Tuition Waiver: All academic, administrative, and enrollment fees are completely covered.
  • Monthly Living Stipend: A generous grant to cover accommodation, food, and daily living expenses.
  • Travel & Mobility Grant: Financial support for airfare, local travel, and visa processing fees.
  • Health Insurance: Full medical and accident insurance coverage throughout the two-year duration.
  • Dual or Joint Degrees: Graduate with recognized qualifications from premier European academic institutions.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for this transformative scholarship, candidates must fulfill the following general requirements:

  • Academic Background: Hold a recognized Bachelor’s degree (minimum 3 or 4 years) in social sciences, economics, law, or related disciplines.
  • Academic Performance: Demonstrate strong academic merit and a clear interest in public policy issues.
  • Language Proficiency: Provide proof of English language proficiency (such as IELTS or TOEFL scores).
  • Inclusivity: Open to applicants of all nationalities, with special encouragement for candidates from Pakistan and the Global South.

Tips for a Successful Application

Competing for a fully funded European master's requires strategic preparation. Draft a compelling Statement of Purpose highlighting how your public policy goals will drive positive social change in your home region. Secure strong academic recommendation letters early, showcase relevant internship or leadership experience, and clearly articulate your commitment to global governance.

Take the leap towards becoming a global policy change-maker. Prepare your application materials early and take your place among the next generation of international leaders!
