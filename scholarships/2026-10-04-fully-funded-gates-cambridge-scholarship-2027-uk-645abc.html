Fully funded Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027, UK

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027, funded by the Gates Cambridge Trust, provides full financial support for international postgraduate students at the University of Cambridge. For the 2027/28 academic year, the program expects to award around 75 full-cost scholarships. This total includes approximately 55 awards distributed through the international selection round and around 20 awards through the US round. About two-thirds of the available funding is reserved for PhD students.

Who Can Apply

The scholarship is open to citizens of non-UK countries applying for PhD programs or select master’s degrees at Cambridge. Specific eligible master's degrees are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Admission is highly competitive, and candidates are evaluated on the following four core criteria:

  • Outstanding academic ability and relevant preparation
  • A clear, logical reason for choosing their specific Cambridge program
  • A proven commitment to improving the lives of others through research, community work, or other initiatives
  • Demonstrated leadership capacity and the ability to motivate others

Benefits

Recipients receive a comprehensive financial package worth approximately £35,000 to £68,000 annually, depending on individual student circumstances. Benefits covered by the scholarship include:

  • The University Composition Fee
  • A maintenance allowance for living expenses
  • Academic development funding (in eligible cases)
  • Dependant Children Allowance (where applicable)

Additional funding components are discretionary. Independent research expenses, such as laboratory consumables, are not funded as a separate line item because they are expected to be covered under the University composition fee.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must apply using the University of Cambridge’s online postgraduate application portal, as the scholarship application is completed directly alongside the admission application. Applicants must submit required documentation, including personal statements, academic transcripts, references, and relevant course materials. Main funding deadlines for 2027/28 entry fall on 8 December 2026 and 6 January 2027, depending on the specific degree program chosen.
