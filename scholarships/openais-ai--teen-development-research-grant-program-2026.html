OpenAI’s AI & Teen Development Research Grant Program 2026

Published by AI Pipeline | September 19, 2026

As generative AI rapidly integrates into education, creativity, and daily life, understanding its profound impact on younger generations has never been more critical. OpenAI has officially launched the AI & Teen Development Research Grant Program 2026, committing $5 million in direct funding to empower independent researchers worldwide. This groundbreaking initiative invites scholars and innovators to explore how artificial intelligence shapes the cognitive, social, and emotional development of teenagers aged 13 to 17.

What is Included

This substantial funding initiative offers comprehensive support to advance rigorous, independent research. Selected grant recipients will benefit from:

  • Access to a share of the total $5 million grant pool to conduct high-impact longitudinal or cross-sectional studies.
  • Financial coverage for primary data collection, research tools, research assistant compensation, and publication fees.
  • A platform to directly inform responsible AI safety protocols, national educational policies, and global child-safety frameworks.
  • Enhanced visibility and networking opportunities within academic, technological, and youth-advocacy communities.

Eligibility Criteria

OpenAI seeks diverse, forward-thinking scholars dedicated to ethical research and youth well-being. Eligible applicants generally include:

  • Independent researchers, university faculty, and research leads affiliated with accredited academic institutions.
  • Non-profit organizations, think tanks, and specialized institutes focused on adolescent psychology, education, or technology.
  • Interdisciplinary research teams combining expertise in computer science, sociology, ethics, and developmental psychology.
  • Projects demonstrating a clear, ethical methodology targeted specifically at understanding teens aged 13–17.

How to Apply

If you are passionate about pioneering safe and beneficial AI ecosystems for youth, prepare your research submission with precision:

  • Develop a robust research proposal detailing your core research questions, target demographics, methodology, and expected outcomes.
  • Outline a realistic budget request showing how grant funds will directly advance your proposed research timeline.
  • Submit your full proposal and institutional credentials through the official OpenAI application portal prior to the deadline on October 6, 2026.
