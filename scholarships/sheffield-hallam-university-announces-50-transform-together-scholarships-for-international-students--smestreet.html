Sheffield Hallam University announces 50% Transform Together Scholarships for international students - SMEStreet

Published by AI Pipeline | September 24, 2026

Embarking on a higher education journey in the United Kingdom is a life-changing milestone, and Sheffield Hallam University is helping talented minds realize their academic ambitions through the prestigious Transform Together Scholarships. Designed specifically for high-achieving international and Pakistani students, this award aims to empower future global leaders who demonstrate academic excellence and strong leadership potential.

Please note that this opportunity is a partially funded scholarship designed to significantly reduce the cost of studying abroad.

What is Included

The Transform Together Scholarship provides valuable financial support and academic prestige to international scholars:

  • A generous 50% tuition fee waiver for postgraduate taught courses or for each year of an undergraduate degree.
  • Opportunities to represent Sheffield Hallam as an official international student ambassador.
  • Enhancement of your CV with a globally recognized merit award from a top UK institution.
  • Access to vibrant campus life, industry partnerships, and advanced academic facilities.

Eligibility Criteria

To be considered for this prestigious partial award, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Be categorized as an international fee-paying applicant (students from Pakistan and all global non-EU countries are encouraged to apply).
  • Hold an active offer (conditional or unconditional) for a full-time taught undergraduate or postgraduate course at Sheffield Hallam University.
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic records from previous qualifications.
  • Fulfill the university's English language proficiency requirements (e.g., IELTS or equivalent).
  • Show a clear record of leadership qualities and a strong vision for your future career.

Application Guidance & Next Steps

To maximize your chances, apply early for your chosen degree course at Sheffield Hallam. Once you secure your student offer, complete the dedicated Transform Together Scholarship application form online. Prepare a persuasive personal statement highlighting your academic achievements, leadership accomplishments, and how you plan to contribute to the university community. Take action today and step confidently toward your academic goals in the UK!
