Gates Cambridge Originals: Martin Kaonga - Gates Cambridge

Published by AI Pipeline | September 23, 2026

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship stands as one of the most prestigious and competitive international awards in higher education. Established by a landmark donation from the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, this program empowers exceptional applicants from outside the United Kingdom—including ambitious students from Pakistan and across the globe—to pursue full-time postgraduate degrees at the world-renowned University of Cambridge. Inspiring journeys like that of scholar Martin Kaonga demonstrate how Gates Cambridge nurtures visionaries dedicated to solving global challenges.

Funding Type: This is a Fully Funded scholarship covering all academic and living expenses for the duration of your course.

What is Included

As a Gates Cambridge Scholar, you will receive full financial coverage designed to let you focus entirely on your academic and leadership potential:

  • University Composition Fee: Full payment of tuition fees at the appropriate international rate.
  • Maintenance Allowance: A generous living stipend (around £20,000 annually for 12-month courses) to comfortably support your stay.
  • Airfare Support: One economy single airfare at both the beginning and end of your program.
  • Visa & Health Costs: Full coverage of inbound UK visa fees and the International Health Surcharge (IHS).
  • Discretionary Funding: Access to additional funding for academic development, field research, family allowances, and hardship support.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for this transformative award, applicants must satisfy the following core requirements:

  • Citizenship: Candidates must be citizens of any country outside the United Kingdom (Pakistani students are eligible).
  • Academic Programs: Must apply for a full-time postgraduate degree (PhD, MSc/MLitt, or a one-year postgraduate course) at Cambridge.
  • Academic Excellence: Demonstrable outstanding intellectual capacity and academic distinction.
  • Leadership & Commitment: Strong leadership potential combined with a compelling commitment to improving the lives of others.

How to Apply

Applying is integrated into the main University of Cambridge graduate admissions process. When submitting your course application via the University Postgraduate Admissions Portal, complete the designated Gates Cambridge section. You will need to provide a dedicated personal statement outlining your commitment to social impact and submit an additional reference highlighting your leadership capabilities.
