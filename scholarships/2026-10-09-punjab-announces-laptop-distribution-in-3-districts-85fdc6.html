Punjab Announces Laptop Distribution in 3 Districts

Published on October 09, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Punjab government has announced plans to distribute Honahar Scholarships and laptops to students across all nine divisions of the province during October and November. According to a social media announcement by Punjab Education Minister Rana Sikandar Hayat, Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz will kick off the initiative on October 15 at the University of Sahiwal. This initial ceremony will bring together students from Sahiwal, Okara, and Pakpattan districts, with similar distribution events planned across the remaining divisions over the following weeks.

Who Can Apply

The program is intended for students across all nine divisions of Punjab, beginning with those located in the Sahiwal, Okara, and Pakpattan districts. Specific academic qualifications, age limits, and eligibility requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients under this provincial initiative receive:

  • Honahar Scholarships
  • Laptops

The specific financial value of the scholarships and the exact number of laptops or awards distributed per division are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
