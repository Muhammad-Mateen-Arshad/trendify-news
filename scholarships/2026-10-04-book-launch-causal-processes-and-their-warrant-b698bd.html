Scholarship Overview

The Atlantic Fellows for Social and Economic Equity (AFSEE) fellowship is a fully-funded programme hosted at the International Inequalities Institute at the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE). This initiative brings together individuals from across the globe to explore and challenge the primary causes of social and economic inequality. The source text also references the Erasmus Scholarship in connection with master's degree studies at the University of Gothenburg.

Who Can Apply

The AFSEE fellowship welcomes applicants from around the world who work across various sectors dedicated to equity, including:

  • Policy-makers
  • Activists
  • Practitioners
  • Artists
  • Movement-builders

Benefits

Selected participants join a transformative community focused on creating equitable alternatives to current global inequalities. The AFSEE programme is designated as a fully-funded fellowship.
