Germany offers fully funded DAAD EPOS scholarships to Pakistani students

Published on October 06, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Germany is offering the fully funded DAAD EPOS scholarship to support qualified young professionals from eligible developing and newly industrialised countries, including Pakistan. The program funds development-related master’s and PhD degrees at designated German higher education institutions. Among the available options, Pakistani applicants can pursue a two-year MSc in Environmental Governance at the University of Freiburg, which is scheduled to begin in October 2027.

Benefits

The scholarship funding package includes the following provisions:

  • A monthly stipend of €992 for master’s level students and up to €1,400 for PhD candidates.
  • Coverage for health, personal liability, and accident insurance.
  • A travel allowance, provided expenses are not paid by the applicant's home country or another organization.
  • A monthly subsidy toward rent under specific circumstances.
  • Supplementary allowances for eligible accompanying family members.
  • Exemption from the €1,500 semester tuition fee usually charged to non-EU students under specified program terms.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible for consideration, applicants must meet these requirements:

  • Be a young professional from an eligible developing or newly industrialised nation such as Pakistan.
  • Hold an accredited university degree, with the bachelor’s degree generally obtained within the six years prior to application.
  • Have acquired at least two years of relevant post-bachelor's work experience. Internships, voluntary work, and typically academic teaching or research do not satisfy this requirement.
  • Maintain an above-average academic standing, typically placing in the upper third of their graduating class.
  • For the MSc in Environmental Governance, hold a bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline (such as environmental sciences, political science, law, economics, agriculture, forestry, or development studies) with a German grade equivalent of 2.5 or better.
  • Meet the university's English-language proficiency criteria.

How to Apply

Applicants must carefully examine all program-specific criteria and assemble the required documentation before submitting their applications. The deadline to apply is October 15, 2026. The exact application submission steps are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
