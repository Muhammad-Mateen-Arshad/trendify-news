World Bank Group Small AI for Development Hackathon Challenge 2026

Published by AI Pipeline | September 20, 2026

Welcome, aspiring innovators, researchers, and changemakers! The World Bank Group has officially launched the Small AI for Development Hackathon Challenge 2026, offering a premier platform to address some of the world's most pressing socio-economic hurdles. With over two billion people still offline and 1.2 billion youth in the Global South entering a job market projected to create only 420 million roles, the need for scalable, lightweight technology has never been more urgent. This challenge empowers you to leverage "Small AI"—efficient, low-resource algorithms—to drive sustainable development where it is needed most.

What is Included

Participating in this transformative global initiative provides extraordinary benefits designed to propel your innovation forward:

  • Global Visibility: Showcase your solution directly to the World Bank Group, international tech leaders, and development policymakers.
  • Expert Mentorship: Access personalized guidance from world-class experts in artificial intelligence, economic development, and digital infrastructure.
  • Funding and Acceleration: Compete for potential cash prizes, seed funding, and implementation grants to help scale your project in target regions.
  • Networking Opportunities: Connect with a global network of like-minded innovators, potential investors, and international NGOs.

Eligibility Criteria

The World Bank Group welcomes diverse, forward-thinking applicants who meet the following guidelines:

  • Open to students, developers, researchers, startups, and tech enthusiasts worldwide, with strong encouragement for candidates from the Global South.
  • Submissions must feature lightweight, resource-efficient AI models designed to operate effectively in low-bandwidth, offline, or resource-constrained settings.
  • Projects must target actionable solutions in critical development areas, such as youth employment, education, agriculture, or healthcare access.
  • Individuals and cross-disciplinary teams are equally eligible to participate.

How to Apply

Taking your idea from concept to global execution involves a straightforward process:

  • Prepare Your Concept: Define your "Small AI" model, focusing on practical applicability, cost-efficiency, and potential impact.
  • Assemble Your Team: Build a balanced team combining technical AI skills with local context or domain expertise.
  • Submit Official Application: Complete the online submission form on the official World Bank Group portal prior to the deadline on September 26, 2026.
