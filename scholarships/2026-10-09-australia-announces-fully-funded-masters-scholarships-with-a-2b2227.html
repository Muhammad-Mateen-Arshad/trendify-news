Australia Announces Fully-Funded Master’s Scholarships With AUD $40,900 Stipend

Published on October 09, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Macquarie University in Australia is accepting applications for its Road to Research Scholarship 2027. Designed for candidates enrolling in a Master of Research (MRes) program, this funding scheme supports students starting their full-time studies in Session 1, January 2027 across various academic fields including business, engineering, law, health, IT, and social sciences.

Benefits

Successful candidates receive comprehensive financial coverage for up to one year, which includes:

  • A full tuition fee offset for up to one year
  • An annual living stipend of AUD 40,900 (based on the 2027 rate)
  • Possibility of stipend top-up funding worth up to 100% of the primary allowance
  • Permission to hold approved part-time employment and receive minor supplementary awards
  • Separate funding options for research expenses like equipment and fieldwork

Who Can Apply

To be considered for the scholarship, candidates must fulfill the following criteria:

  • Be physically onshore in Australia at the time of application submission and plan to reside in Sydney during their research candidature.
  • Begin the Master of Research full-time in January 2027.
  • Satisfy Macquarie University’s academic entry and English-language requirements.
  • International candidates (including Pakistani students) may apply provided they are already onshore in Australia and hold valid visa/admission status.
  • Current Macquarie postgraduate coursework students finishing in Session 2, 2026 are also eligible.

Selection is based on a competitive ranking process using the candidate's Weighted Average Mark (WAM) or Grade Point Average (GPA) for qualifications completed prior to 2020 or at external institutions.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their application online for the Session 1, 2027 intake before October 31, 2026, at 11:00 PM AEDT. Results are expected in early December 2026. Applicants should prepare the following documents:

  • Academic transcripts and degree completion documents
  • A personal essay or supporting statement
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Accurate visa and residency details (for overseas candidates)
  • Confirmation of supervision from an eligible Macquarie researcher (mandatory for the Faculty of Medicine, Health and Human Sciences; strongly recommended for other faculties)
