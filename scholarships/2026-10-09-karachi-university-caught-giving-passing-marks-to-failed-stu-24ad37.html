Karachi University Caught Giving Passing Marks to Failed Students

Published on October 09, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The provided source does not contain details regarding a scholarship opportunity. Instead, it reports on allegations concerning the University of Karachi and its examination review process. According to the report, around 150 students from affiliated law schools, including S.M. Law College and Islamia Law College, were given passing marks after initially failing their BA LLB exams in subjects such as Obligations and Pakistan Studies. The grade revisions were authorized by then-acting Vice Chancellor Dr. Tufail Jokhio following a committee's recommendations. Former Vice Chancellor Dr. Khalid Iraqi noted that annual examination rules allow for recounting marks rather than rechecking answers, while S.M. Law College Principal Prof. Fareed Dayo defended the decision, stating it provided relief to students who faced unfair evaluation.
