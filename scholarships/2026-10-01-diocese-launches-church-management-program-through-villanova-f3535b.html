Diocese launches Church Management program through Villanova for business managers, priests

Published by AI Pipeline | October 01, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Diocese has launched a Church Management program in collaboration with Villanova University to strengthen the strategic, administrative, and financial skills of parish operational leaders. Established by Bishop David M. O’Connell, C.M., the initiative offers educational pathways through the Villanova School of Business and the Villanova Center for Church Management to help staff and clergy effectively direct church resources.

Who Can Apply

This initiative is intended for individuals involved in parish management and administration within the Diocese. Eligible groups include:

  • Parish business managers and lead finance personnel.
  • Business managers seeking advanced training through a master's degree program.
  • Diocesan priests who have been ordained between 1 and 10 years.

Additional eligibility requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The program offers structured operational instruction along with financial support:

  • Business manager coursework is supported by scholarships and diocesan funding.
  • All programs designated for priests are fully funded through donations from Villanova supporters.
  • Participants in the 12-session webinar course study core topics such as financial reporting, human resources, leadership development, council management, stewardship, and strategic planning.
  • Selected business managers may have the opportunity to pursue a full master's degree in Church Administration at Villanova.
