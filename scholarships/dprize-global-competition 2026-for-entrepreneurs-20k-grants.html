D-Prize Global Competition 2026 for Entrepreneurs ($20K grants)

Published by AI Pipeline | September 19, 2026

Are you an aspiring entrepreneur dedicated to tackling global inequality? The D-Prize Global Competition 2026 offers an extraordinary launching pad to turn your vision into a sustainable, high-impact reality. D-Prize believes the world desperately needs more social impact organizations capable of delivering proven, life-saving interventions directly to communities in need. Whether you are launching a for-profit social enterprise, an NGO, or a charity, this competition provides the vital initial momentum required to fight extreme poverty.

What is Included

Selected winners receive comprehensive support to help their ventures transition from concept to operational success:

  • Seed Capital: Grants of up to $20,000 to fund your initial pilot project and prove your distribution concept in the field.
  • Flexible Funding Model: Financial support catered to your chosen organizational structure, whether for-profit, non-profit, or hybrid.
  • Global Mentorship: Direct access to an international network of social leaders, customized operational guidance, and long-term scaling opportunities.

Eligibility Criteria

The competition welcomes passionate change-makers globally who meet the following core requirements:

  • Geographic Focus: Initiatives must serve communities within low- or middle-income nations where extreme poverty is present.
  • Stage of Venture: Open to new, early-stage organizations or founders ready to launch their very first operational pilot.
  • Impact-Driven Approach: Your proposal must center on efficiently distributing established, evidence-based solutions in sectors like health, education, agriculture, clean energy, or economic empowerment.

How to Apply

Transforming your ambition into action is a simple, structured process:

  • Select a Challenge: Review the official D-Prize distribution challenges or present your own original proposal addressing extreme poverty.
  • Prepare Your Proposal: Write a concise, execution-focused plan detailing your operational strategies, target audience, and pilot goals.
  • Submit Early: Finalize and submit your online application package before the official deadline on December 6, 2026.

Your leadership and innovative thinking can create lasting, transformative change for thousands of families. Take the leap, submit your application today, and begin building a venture that truly transforms the world!
