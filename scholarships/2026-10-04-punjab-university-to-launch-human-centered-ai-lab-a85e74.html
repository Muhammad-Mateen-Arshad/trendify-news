Punjab University to Launch Human-Centered AI Lab

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Punjab University’s Faculty of Computing and Information Technology is inaugurating a Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence Laboratory as part of a broader research and academic partnership between Pakistan and Türkiye. The facility was established through joint cooperation among Punjab University, Türkiye’s Ministry of Culture and Tourism, and the Turkish Cooperation and Coordination Agency (TIKA). To further support this collaboration, Punjab University has also entered into an academic and research cooperation agreement with Yildiz Technical University in Türkiye. The initiative aims to facilitate joint artificial intelligence research, academic exchange, and knowledge sharing between the two nations.

The academic exchange and research opportunities are intended for researchers, faculty members, and students from Pakistan and Türkiye.
