HEC Shares Lists of Recognized and Unrecognized Institutions

Published on October 08, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) has published official lists detailing recognized and unrecognized educational institutions. This advisory urges parents and prospective students to thoroughly verify universities and regional campuses before submitting admission applications. Degrees from institutions that are omitted from the officially recognized lists will not be acknowledged by the HEC. Furthermore, the commission warned students against enrolling in degree-awarding bodies that are currently facing admission bans as a result of administrative irregularities.

How to Apply

Students should review the designated directories provided by the HEC prior to applying for academic programs. The commission has categorized the guidance into four separate lists:

  • Recognized universities
  • Approved campuses
  • Transnational education providers
  • Illegal or fake institutions
