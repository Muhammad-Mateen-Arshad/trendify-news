1834 Global Fellowship 2027 for Black Canadians

Published by AI Pipeline | September 21, 2026

Are you a young Black Canadian passionate about shaping foreign policy, international development, or global governance? The 1834 Global Fellowship 2027 represents an unprecedented launchpad designed to empower the next generation of civic and international leaders. This transformative 9-week program bridges the gap between local ambition and high-level global impact, equipping high-potential youth with the essential tools, professional networks, and policy insights needed to excel on the world stage.

What is Included

Selected fellows undergo a comprehensive and dynamic curriculum curated to elevate their career trajectory in public policy and global affairs. The program features:

  • Expert Mentorship: One-on-one guidance and strategic career advice from seasoned diplomats, foreign policy experts, and international leaders.
  • Targeted Skill Building: Intensive workshops focusing on policy analysis, cross-cultural negotiation, strategic communication, and international advocacy.
  • Interactive Virtual Mixers: High-impact networking sessions connecting fellows with prominent global institutions, think tanks, and like-minded peers.
  • Career Acceleration: Tailored support designed to help participants navigate and secure leadership roles in global civic engagement.

Eligibility Criteria

To be considered for this prestigious leadership initiative, applicants must fulfill the following requirements:

  • Identify as a Black Canadian.
  • Be between 20 and 30 years of age.
  • Demonstrate a strong track record of high potential and a genuine passion for global civic affairs, international relations, or diplomacy.
  • Be fully committed to actively engaging in all online sessions, mentorship meetings, and program activities over the 9-week fellowship period.

How to Apply

Stepping into international leadership begins with a strong, well-prepared application. Follow these key steps to apply:

  • Update your resume or CV to emphasize your academic background, community involvement, and leadership experiences.
  • Craft a compelling personal statement outlining your dedication to international affairs and how this fellowship aligns with your long-term goals.
  • Submit your completed online application before the official deadline on October 13, 2026.
