Chevening Scholarship 2027-2028: Application guide for Bangladeshi students

Published on October 04, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Chevening Scholarship offers emerging leaders from around the world the opportunity to undertake a fully funded, one-year master’s degree in the United Kingdom. For Bangladeshi students, this award aims to support individuals who demonstrate leadership skills, influence, and a strong commitment to tackling significant local, national, or global issues. It allows recipients to build professional networks and advance their academic expertise while studying in the UK.

Who Can Apply

Bangladeshi candidates must meet specific criteria to be eligible for consideration:

  • Hold citizenship in a Chevening-eligible country or territory, and reside in an ODA-eligible country where applicable.
  • Commit to returning to Bangladesh for at least two years following the conclusion of the scholarship.
  • Possess at least two years of work experience (equivalent to 2,800 hours) earned after completing an undergraduate degree.
  • Hold an undergraduate degree that qualifies them for a UK master’s program, which must have been completed a minimum of two years prior to the application deadline.
  • Apply to three distinct eligible master’s courses at UK universities.
  • Obtain an unconditional offer from at least one of these chosen courses by the specified deadline.

Benefits

Recipients receive full funding to pursue a one-year master’s degree program in the UK. Exact funding amounts and stipend breakdowns are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Applicants from Bangladesh should process their applications online through the Chevening system. The application steps include:

  • Answering the initial eligibility questions on the official application portal.
  • Researching suitable UK master's programs and submitting applications to three eligible courses.
  • Preparing and submitting the official scholarship application before the deadline. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • If selected for an interview, uploading necessary supporting materials—such as photo ID, undergraduate certificate or diploma, and reference letters—no later than seven working days prior to the interview.
