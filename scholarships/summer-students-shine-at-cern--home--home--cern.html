Summer students shine at CERN – Home - Home | CERN

Published by AI Pipeline | September 22, 2026

Are you an ambitious student in physics, engineering, computer science, or mathematics dreaming of conducting research at the world’s leading particle physics laboratory? The prestigious CERN Summer Student Program in Geneva, Switzerland, offers an extraordinary opportunity to work alongside world-class scientists and engineers. Designed to cultivate the next generation of scientific leaders, this program welcomes talented undergraduate and master’s students from around the globe, including Pakistan and other international countries.

Program Overview & Funding Status

This program is FULLY FUNDED. CERN ensures that selected students can focus entirely on their research and professional development without financial stress. Participants spend 8 to 13 weeks immersed in groundbreaking scientific experiments, attending specialized lectures, and networking with a global community of innovators.

What is Included

  • Daily Subsistence Allowance: A generous per diem to comfortable cover accommodation, meals, and living expenses in Geneva.
  • Travel Grant: Coverage for round-trip travel costs from your home country to Switzerland.
  • Comprehensive Health Insurance: CERN health coverage for the entire duration of the program.
  • Academic Growth: Access to a specialized lecture series delivered by world-renowned experts, along with hands-on technical project work.
  • Assistance: Full guidance in securing necessary visas and housing arrangements.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Academic Standing: Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in Physics, Engineering, Computer Science, or Mathematics.
  • Progress: Must have completed at least 3 years of full-time university-level studies by the start of the summer program.
  • Language Skills: High proficiency in English is required; knowledge of French is an added advantage but not mandatory.
  • Nationality: Open to candidates of all nationalities. Students from CERN Member and Associate Member States (including Pakistan) are strongly encouraged to apply.

Why You Should Apply

Participating in the CERN Summer Student Program is a transformative milestone for any academic CV. Beyond gaining invaluable technical skills, you will experience an inspiring international environment that opens doors to top-tier global graduate schools and careers. Do not let this chance pass by—prepare your transcripts, recommendation letters, and motivation statement today!
