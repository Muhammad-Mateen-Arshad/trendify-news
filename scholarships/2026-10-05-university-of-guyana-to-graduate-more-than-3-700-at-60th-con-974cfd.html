University of Guyana to Graduate More Than 3,700 at 60th Convocation

Published on October 05, 2026

Scholarship Overview

State education initiatives in Guyana support higher learning through substantial government funding and scholarship programs. Tuition at the University of Guyana became free starting January 1, 2025, benefiting more than 11,000 students. In addition, the government funds distance education through the Guyana Online Academy of Learning (GOAL), which partners with over 40 international academic institutions. Out of Guyana's 2026 national education budget of G$183.6 billion (US$878 million), G$5.8 billion (US$28 million) was allocated to GOAL, while the University of Guyana received G$14.5 billion (US$69 million). GOAL had awarded 54,790 scholarships by June 2026.

The University of Guyana is hosting its 60th convocation in November to confer degrees, diplomas, and certificates on over 3,700 graduating students across its main Turkeyen campus, Berbice campus, and distance education centers. All graduation ceremonies will be streamed online via the university's Facebook page and its broadcasting service.

Benefits

Educational funding benefits mentioned in the national support system include:

  • Free university tuition for students attending the University of Guyana since January 2025.
  • Fully funded online scholarship opportunities provided through GOAL in collaboration with more than 40 international partner institutions.
  • Academic degree, diploma, and certificate offerings across fields such as engineering, entrepreneurship, natural sciences, medical sciences, agriculture, humanities, and accredited petroleum engineering.
  • Expanded educational access across all regions, including specialized pre-medicine offerings.
