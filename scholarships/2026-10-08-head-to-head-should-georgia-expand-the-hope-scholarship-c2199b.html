Head to Head: Should Georgia expand the HOPE scholarship?

Published on October 08, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Georgia's HOPE Scholarship is a long-standing, merit-based financial aid program created in 1993 to lower higher education costs and encourage talented students to remain in Georgia for college. Since its inception, the initiative has supported over 2.2 million students and delivered more than $16 billion in financial assistance. The program consistently reaches over 100,000 students annually. In fiscal year 2024, more than $50 million was awarded to 103,116 students attending schools within the University System of Georgia and the Technical College System of Georgia.

By offering tuition support, the scholarship addresses rising educational expenses, where public four-year university in-state tuition averages over $11,500. Additionally, the program helps build Georgia's workforce by preparing students for high-demand careers in healthcare, engineering, and education.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility for the HOPE Scholarship is based on academic achievement rather than financial status. Requirements include:

  • Graduating high school with at least a 3.0 GPA.
  • Maintaining a minimum 3.0 GPA while enrolled in college.
  • Attending an eligible in-state public college or university in Georgia.

Benefits

Recipients of the HOPE Scholarship receive several key financial advantages:

  • Direct funding toward in-state tuition costs at public colleges and technical institutions in Georgia.
  • Consistent financial relief that assists middle-income and high-achieving students in avoiding heavy education debt.
  • Access to reliable merit-based funding that remains fully funded year after year.
