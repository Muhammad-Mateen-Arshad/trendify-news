Cheapest Countries for a Master’s Degree for Pakistani Students — 2026 Update

Published by AI Pipeline | October 03, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Pakistani students seeking a Master’s degree abroad during the 2026–2027 academic years can pursue either self-funded study or fully funded scholarships. Fully funded awards help reduce the financial burden by covering tuition and primary living costs. Key international locations highlighted for manageable costs or scholarship opportunities include Germany, Hungary, Türkiye, Malaysia, Poland, the Czech Republic, and the United States.

Who Can Apply

Specific qualifications like age limits or academic grade minimums are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, general applicant requirements mentioned in the text include:

  • Pakistani students aiming for Master's programs overseas.
  • Fulfillment of language conditions, such as IELTS or a Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificate.
  • Adherence to Higher Education Commission (HEC) standards where required.

Benefits

Financial support and cost structures vary according to the chosen nation and program:

  • Fully Funded Programs: Cover total tuition costs alongside major living expenses.
  • Germany: Most public universities do not charge tuition to non-EU students, though applicants need to handle living expenses and open a blocked account containing approximately €11,900 per year.
  • Hungary: Offers scholarship options through the Stipendium Hungaricum program.
  • Türkiye: Provides financial pathways via the Türkiye Bursları scholarship.
  • Exact scholarship allowance amounts for individual programs are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Prospective students should begin their preparations early rather than waiting for the final application month. Recommended application steps include:

  • Collect all necessary degree certificates and academic transcripts.
  • Research and identify matching universities and degree programs.
  • Draft a motivation letter or statement of purpose.
  • Check and fulfill English language requirements (such as IELTS or MOI) and review any applicable HEC criteria.
  • Submit applications for Hungarian scholarships directly through the HEC platform.
  • For the Türkiye Bursları scholarship, keep in mind the cited application period from 10 January to 20 February 2027.
  • Send in all completed application forms well ahead of official deadlines.
