Imperial College London PhD Scholarship Opens With £27,036 Annual Stipend

Published by AI Pipeline | October 03, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Imperial College London in the United Kingdom has opened applications for the President’s PhD Scholarship for the 2027-28 academic year. A total of 50 scholarships will be awarded to high-achieving doctoral candidates worldwide. The award enables selected students to undertake doctoral research under the guidance of an eligible academic supervisor, with degree start dates falling between August 1 and November 1, 2027.

Who Can Apply

The scholarship is available to both domestic UK candidates and international applicants, including students from Pakistan, without any nationality restrictions. Eligibility criteria include:

  • Candidates pursuing either full-time or part-time PhD studies can apply.
  • Applicants generally require a first-class undergraduate degree, an integrated Master’s degree, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Students applying with a standalone Master’s degree are expected to have earned a distinction.
  • The funding is restricted to new PhD applicants; currently registered PhD students at Imperial are ineligible.
  • Candidates must satisfy all standard entry requirements for their chosen academic department and doctoral program.

Benefits

Successful candidates receive comprehensive funding and academic support, which includes:

  • Complete tuition fee coverage for 3.5 years.
  • An annual stipend of £27,036 to cover living expenses.
  • An additional £2,000 per year for research consumables during the first three years.
  • Access to specialized training and development programs via Imperial’s Early Career Researcher Institute.

How to Apply

There is no separate application form for the scholarship. Candidates must carry out the following procedure:

  • Contact an eligible Imperial academic early to secure their agreement to supervise the proposed research topic.
  • Submit a standard PhD admission application through Imperial's online admissions portal.
  • Indicate interest by selecting the President’s PhD Scholarship option in the funding section of the application form.

Applications for the 2027-28 intake are evaluated across three distinct selection cycles:

  • Round 1: Deadline is November 2, 2026 (Decisions by January 31, 2027).
  • Round 2: Deadline is January 11, 2027 (Decisions by April 15, 2027).
  • Round 3: Deadline is March 1, 2027 (Decisions by May 31, 2027).

Applicants who are not awarded the scholarship may still be considered for standard PhD admission at Imperial College London.
