Canada: Basant Motors awards $35,000 in scholarships to 17 students on its 35th anniversary in Surrey

Published on October 10, 2026

Scholarship Overview

To celebrate its 35th anniversary in Surrey, Canada, Basant Motors awarded $35,000 in scholarships to 17 deserving students. The company's annual education initiative is designed to offer financial assistance and motivate young individuals to pursue higher education and fulfill their academic ambitions. With this latest distribution, the program has provided a cumulative total of $360,000 to 146 student beneficiaries since its inception.

The scholarship event brought together academics, radio hosts, community leaders, and families. Key presenters included former Pakistani MP Rai Aziz Ullah Khan, Harjinder Singh Thind, Prof. Kashmira Singh, Sucha Singh Kaler, B.S. Ghuman, Dr. Stalanjit Singh Gill, Jatinder J. Minhas, and Vijay Saini. Addressing the gathering, Baldev Singh Bath expressed gratitude to the community and parents, emphasizing the role of collective support in achieving success and confirming the organization's commitment to continuing the scholarship program in the future.
