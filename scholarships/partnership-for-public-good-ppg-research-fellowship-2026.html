Partnership for Public Good (PPG) Research Fellowship 2026

Published by AI Pipeline | September 21, 2026

Welcome aspiring changemakers and policy scholars! If you are an early-career researcher passionate about transforming governance and driving meaningful social impact, the Partnership for Public Good (PPG) Research Fellowship 2026 presents an extraordinary career opportunity. This prestigious six-month, project-based program empowers emerging scholars to tackle pressing policy challenges and enhance government effectiveness through rigorous, evidence-based research.

What is Included

As a PPG Fellow, you will gain invaluable practical experience while receiving financial support to focus entirely on your project. Key benefits of the fellowship include:

  • Competitive Financial Compensation: A paid fellowship designed to support researchers throughout the six-month duration.
  • Hands-on Research Experience: Direct involvement in policy-relevant background research and real-world data collection.
  • Professional Development: Mentorship from seasoned policy experts and exposure to government networks.
  • Actionable Impact: A platform to publish data-driven findings that directly inform and improve public sector performance.

Eligibility Criteria

The fellowship targets dedicated scholars eager to bridge the gap between academic research and real-world policy implementation. Applicants should meet the following criteria:

  • Be an early-career researcher, graduate student, or recent graduate with a strong academic background in public policy, economics, political science, or related disciplines.
  • Demonstrate a keen interest in improving government effectiveness and public administration.
  • Possess strong skills in rigorous research methodologies, including qualitative and quantitative data collection and analysis.
  • Exhibit excellent analytical, writing, and communication abilities.

How to Apply

Securing this fellowship requires a strategic application that highlights your analytical capabilities and commitment to public service. Follow these steps to prepare:

  • Prepare Your Documents: Draft an up-to-date curriculum vitae (CV), a tailored cover letter detailing your research interests, and writing samples showcasing your data collection and analytical skills.
  • Highlight Practical Relevance: Clearly articulate how your research background aligns with enhancing government operations and public policy.
  • Submit Early: Complete and submit your online application well before the official deadline on September 28, 2026.
