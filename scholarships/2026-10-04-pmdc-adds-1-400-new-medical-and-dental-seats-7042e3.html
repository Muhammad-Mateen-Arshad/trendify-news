PMDC adds 1,400 New Medical and Dental Seats

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

Scholarship Overview

A total of 1,400 newly approved medical and dental seats have been distributed across provincial and federal quota categories. This distribution covers both MBBS and BDS educational pathways, providing opportunities for students seeking entry into medical and dental institutions under provincial and federal systems.

Who Can Apply

The seat allocation applies to candidates based on their region and quota category. The regional distribution for MBBS seats is structured as follows:

  • Punjab: Allocated 475 MBBS seats.
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP): Allocated 150 MBBS seats.
  • Balochistan: Allocated 50 additional MBBS seats.
  • Sindh: No seats are listed under this announced distribution.
  • Federal Areas: Students from Islamabad, Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK), Gilgit-Baltistan (GB), and other federal quota divisions.

Specific personal or academic eligibility criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

This distribution creates 1,400 learning positions across medical and dental disciplines. A significant portion consists of 725 federal allocation seats, which are broken down into three key areas:

  • 325 seats designated under HEC’s previous FATA and Balochistan scholarship arrangements.
  • 250 seats allocated to the Federal Medical College (FMC), Shaikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Al-Nahyan Medical and Dental College (SKZMDC), and federal dentistry programs.
  • 150 seats set aside under federal quotas in other provinces.
