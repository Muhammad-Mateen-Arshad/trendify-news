3 Fully Funded PhD Scholarships in the US Close September 30

Published by AI Pipeline | September 28, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Three universities in the United States are offering fully funded doctoral opportunities for students interested in environmental science, biological research, and fisheries. Candidates must submit their application materials by September 30, 2026. The participating institutions—Louisiana State University, the University of North Texas, and the University of Vermont—provide financial support reaching up to $37,000 annually.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility criteria and research focus areas vary across the three institutions:

  • Louisiana State University: Applicants should hold or be close to completing a relevant master's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0. The PhD position focuses on wetland and aquatic biogeochemistry, specifically examining phosphorus dynamics and harmful algal blooms within the Lake Pontchartrain Estuary and neighboring waterways.
  • University of North Texas: Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree in biology, ecology, environmental science, entomology, or a related field, with a minimum GPA of 3.5. The selected student will carry out field research throughout North Texas to assess how ranch management impacts insect pollinators, plants, soil microbes, and habitat quality.
  • University of Vermont: Candidates need an academic background in fisheries science, ecology, environmental studies, geography, sociology, anthropology, natural resources, data science, or a related field. Based in the Bernos F.I.S.H. Lab, the assistantship focuses on the human dimensions of fisheries, analyzing interactions between humans, fish, and ecosystems in Lake Champlain. Research topics include fishing behavior, stakeholder preferences, fisheries management, social networks, and fish movement.

Benefits

Funding packages provided by each university include:

  • Louisiana State University: Offers an initial annual stipend of $31,000 and covers 75% of health insurance premiums. The position is slated to start after January 1, 2027.
  • University of North Texas: Provides an annual salary between $26,400 and $28,320, along with tuition coverage and health-related support based on the student's graduate level.
  • University of Vermont: Delivers $37,000 per year, tuition support, and a living allowance. The position begins in January 2027.

How to Apply

Applicants must prepare required submission materials, which include academic transcripts, degree certificates, CVs, statements of interest, and letters of recommendation. Certain positions may require applicants to get in touch with a research supervisor before applying. All documents must be submitted by September 30, 2026.
