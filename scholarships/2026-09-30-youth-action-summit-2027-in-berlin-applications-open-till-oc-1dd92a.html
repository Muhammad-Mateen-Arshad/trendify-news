Youth Action Summit 2027 In Berlin: Applications Open Till October 5, Check Eligibility, Scholarships

Published by AI Pipeline | September 30, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Centre for Diplomatic Advancement (CDA) is accepting applications for the Youth Action Summit (YAS) 2027, taking place in Berlin, Germany, from January 11 to 14, 2027. This four-day international gathering aims to foster global collaboration through diplomacy, policy discussions, leadership development, and cross-cultural exchanges. To support attending participants, the organizers are offering 250 scholarship awards and access passes.

Who Can Apply

The summit welcomes applicants across various educational backgrounds and nationalities. Interested candidates should meet the following requirements:

  • Must be between 16 and 24 years old.
  • Open to students, activists, young professionals, entrepreneurs, and anyone interested in diplomacy and leadership.
  • Must demonstrate a working knowledge of English.
  • Must share details about their motivation and leadership experience in the application.

Previous professional or work experience is not required to be eligible for this program.

Benefits

The summit provides three tiers of funding support:

  • Fully Funded Scholarships (50 places): Covers two-way air travel, shared accommodation at a four-star hotel, visa support, visa fee reimbursement, access to all sessions and workshops, meals, networking events, a delegate kit, and an official certificate from the CDA.
  • Partially Funded Scholarships (100 places): Includes summit access, accommodation, meals, and visa support. Participants are responsible for buying their own plane tickets.
  • Forum Access Passes (100 passes): Grants entry to summit sessions and provides meals. Attendees are required to handle their own travel and lodging costs.

How to Apply

Prospective delegates must complete their application online through the official summit website. The closing date for all submissions is October 5, 2026. Applicants are recommended to thoroughly read through all scholarship conditions and requirements before submitting their materials.
