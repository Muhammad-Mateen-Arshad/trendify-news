3 Fully-Funded PhD Scholarships in the Netherlands Closing in September 2026

Published by AI Pipeline | September 30, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The University of Amsterdam in the Netherlands is inviting applications for three fully-funded PhD research opportunities. These positions cover three distinct academic fields: quantum physics, artificial intelligence in political communication, and marine ecology. Each position offers a four-year PhD employment contract with an application closing date of September 30, 2026.

Who Can Apply

Academic requirements depend on the specific project selected:

  • Quantum Physics: Applicants must hold a recent master's degree in experimental ultracold atom or trapped-ion physics, combined with strong initiative, teamwork, time management, and proficiency in written and spoken English.
  • AI and Political Communication: Candidates need a completed MA or Research MA in political science, psychology, communication science, computational social sciences, sociology, or a related field. Applicants must possess knowledge of AI and emotions, expertise in research methods, and excellent English.
  • Marine Ecology: Applicants require an MSc in ecology (preferably with a theoretical ecology or biology background) or strong quantitative skills in population or food-web modelling, alongside computational abilities and an interest in marine biology or seabird ecology.

Pakistan is explicitly listed among the eligible countries. For details regarding other specific eligible nationalities: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected candidates will be granted full-time research employment contracts that include:

  • A gross monthly salary starting at €3,204 and rising to €4,051 in the final year.
  • An 8% holiday allowance and an 8.3% year-end allowance.
  • Academic supervision, pension benefits, professional training, and opportunities to assist with university teaching.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their applications through the University of Amsterdam's online vacancy system prior to the deadline. Required application materials vary by position but may include a detailed CV with education and work dates, a motivation letter of up to two A4 pages, a writing sample, and the names and email addresses of two references. Applications for the quantum physics position must also be sent by email to Prof. Dr. Florian Schreck.
