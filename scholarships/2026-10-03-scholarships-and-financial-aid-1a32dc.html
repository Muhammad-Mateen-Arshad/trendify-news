Scholarships and Financial Aid

Published by AI Pipeline | October 03, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Luther College, located in Decorah, Iowa, provides financial assistance to full-time students during the academic year. The funding packages combine academic merit scholarships, need-based aid, talent awards, and work-study options. Academic merit scholarships provide up to $39,000 per academic year and can be renewed through a student's fourth year of enrollment. While financial aid is substantial, international students are generally expected to cover a portion of their expenses through personal funds, family contributions, or external organizations.

Who Can Apply

Financial assistance is available to incoming international students and U.S. citizens who reside outside the United States. Specific awards cater to students demonstrating academic excellence, musical skill, or theatrical talent. Additional opportunities exist for dependent children of Luther alumni, applicants to the StudyIowa Anne Doyle Award, and students pursuing art programs.

Benefits

  • Academic merit scholarships worth up to $39,000 per academic year, renewable for up to four years.
  • Guaranteed on-campus employment for participating international students, providing earnings of around $3,800 during the academic year.
  • An award covering estimated health insurance premiums for international students for one year, renewable through the fourth year.
  • Financial support designated for initial textbook costs, school supplies, and personal adjustment expenses for incoming international students.
  • Talent-based awards in disciplines such as music, theatre, and art.

How to Apply

  • Academic merit scholarships do not require a separate form; eligible applicants are automatically evaluated during the admissions review process.
  • International students seeking financial aid must submit a completed International Student Application for Financial Assistance (ISAFA) along with supporting documents, such as family wage reports or certified bank statements.
  • U.S. citizens living abroad should complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) rather than the ISAFA.
  • Auditioned music scholarships require the Music Scholarship Application, whereas non-auditioned music awards require no extra application.
  • Theatre scholarships require a separate submission by March 29, 2027, followed by an audition, interview, or portfolio review by March 31, 2027.
  • For specific deadlines on other individual awards, exact details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
