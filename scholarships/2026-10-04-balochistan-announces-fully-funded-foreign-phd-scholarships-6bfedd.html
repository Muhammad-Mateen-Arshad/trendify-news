Balochistan announces fully funded foreign PhD scholarships

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

The government of Balochistan has launched a new foreign PhD scholarship initiative administered by the Balochistan Education Endowment Fund (BEEF). As reported by ARY News, this program is designed to support high-achieving individuals from the region in pursuing doctoral degrees at recognized international institutions.

Scholarship Overview

Chief Minister Mir Sarfaraz Bugti characterized the program as a landmark achievement for the future of the province, marking the start of a new pathway for local scholars to attend prestigious global universities. The Chief Minister noted that the initiative aims to transform Balochistan's trajectory by ensuring its youth remain competitive with the rest of the world. He framed the project as a beacon of hope, knowledge, and development for future generations.

Additionally, Chief Minister Bugti stressed that offering educational, awareness, and development opportunities serves as the provincial government's strategy to address terrorism. He stated that the true future of Balochistan lies with young people who excel in academic research rather than those involved in armed conflict, with the overarching objective of placing local scholars prominently on the global education map.

Who Can Apply

The candidate details provided in the announcement include:

  • Applicants must be competent and deserving students from the province of Balochistan.
  • A total of ten candidates will be chosen for the award each year.
  • Selected individuals must use the scholarship to study at recognized international universities.
  • Specific age limits, subject disciplines, and minimum academic qualifications are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
