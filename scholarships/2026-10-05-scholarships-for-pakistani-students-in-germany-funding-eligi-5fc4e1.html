Scholarships for Pakistani Students in Germany: Funding, Eligibility and How to Apply

Published on October 05, 2026

Most public universities in Germany do not charge tuition fees for international students. As a result, available funding programs primarily focus on helping Pakistani students manage living expenses, which usually range from €850 to €1,200 per month.

Scholarship Overview

Pakistani scholars can access several funding avenues in Germany, including DAAD awards, the Deutschlandstipendium, and political foundation scholarships. While options for undergraduate students are less common, opportunities exist across bachelor's, master's, and doctoral studies.

Benefits

Funding amounts vary depending on the specific provider and study level:

  • DAAD Scholarships: Provide €1,000 monthly for master's degree students and €1,300 monthly for PhD candidates.
  • Deutschlandstipendium: Offers €300 per month for bachelor's students.
  • Political Foundation Scholarships: Supply between €861 and €1,350 each month.

Additionally, students applying for a German student visa in 2026 must demonstrate financial resources of €11,904 in a blocked account, equaling €992 per month.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements vary depending on the scholarship provider:

  • Academic excellence, leadership potential, or active involvement in social, environmental, or political activities.
  • Specific conditions for certain awards, such as holding a bachelor's degree completed within the last six years, being under 45 years of age, or securing formal acceptance from a German professor for PhD studies.
  • Preference for low-income candidates or applicants with voluntary service experience. Some foundation awards require students to already be enrolled in Germany.
  • Language Requirements: English-taught programs require an IELTS score of 6.5 or a TOEFL iBT score of 90. German-taught options require B2 German proficiency, with C1 expected within the first year.

How to Apply

Follow these steps to complete your application:

  • Select funding options aligned with your study field and academic level.
  • Prepare required materials, including academic transcripts, a CV, a motivation letter, recommendation letters, language credentials, and a research proposal for PhD applicants.
  • Submit your application through the provider's portal, or through your host university for the Deutschlandstipendium.
  • Prepare for an interview if selected by a foundation.

Deadlines for the 2026/27 cycle have passed. Specific dates for future application cycles are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
