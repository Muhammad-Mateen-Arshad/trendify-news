National University of Singapore is Offering PhD Scholarships With S$2,200 Monthly Stipend

Published on October 05, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The National University of Singapore (NUS) offers the Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy PhD Scholarship for candidates enrolling in the PhD in Public Policy program at the Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy (LKYSPP). For the August 2027 intake, the application period runs from 1 August 2026 to 10 December 2026. The scholarship carries a standard award duration of three years, though students are allowed up to five years to complete their full PhD candidature.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must meet specific academic and background criteria to be considered:

  • Eligible Statuses: International applicants (including students from Pakistan), Singapore Permanent Residents, and Singapore Citizens.
  • Academic Background: Candidates generally need a good relevant Master's degree or equivalent qualification related to the social sciences, alongside a clear interest in public policy research and international affairs.
  • Work Experience: Preference is given to applicants who have at least two years of relevant work experience.
  • References: Candidates must provide two academic referees capable of assessing their research potential and academic capability.
  • Language Proficiency: Applicants whose prior education does not fulfill the English language policy may need an English proficiency test. Admitted students commonly achieve around an IELTS 7.0 or equivalent TOEFL score.

Benefits

Financial support is provided through monthly stipends categorized by residency status. All recipients receive an additional S$500 per month after successfully passing their PhD Qualifying Examination:

  • International Students: Receive S$2,200 per month initially, which increases to S$2,700 per month after passing the qualifying exam.
  • Singapore Permanent Residents: Receive S$2,400 per month initially, plus the S$500 monthly increase post-examination.
  • Singapore Citizens: Receive S$2,800 per month initially, plus the S$500 monthly post-examination increase and Central Provident Fund contributions.

Tuition coverage details: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must apply using the NUS Graduate Admission System (GradApp). A separate university scholarship application is not required, as scholarship evaluation is integrated directly into the PhD program application. Applicants must complete the online submission, attach required supporting documents, and ensure referee recommendations are submitted before the closing date.
