Cornell University AI for Sustainability (AI4S) Visiting Professorship Program 2027

Published by AI Pipeline | September 19, 2026

Are you a faculty scholar passionate about leveraging cutting-edge artificial intelligence to solve pressing global environmental challenges? The prestigious Cornell University AI for Sustainability (AI4S) Visiting Professorship Program 2027 offers an extraordinary opportunity to elevate your academic career and drive meaningful global change. Hosted by one of the world's leading Ivy League institutions, this program invites international academics to engage in transformative interdisciplinary research at the intersection of AI and sustainability.

Program Overview & Benefits

Selected visiting professors will immerse themselves in Cornell’s vibrant academic ecosystem. As a fellow, you will enjoy unique opportunities to collaborate, innovate, and lead, including:

  • Direct collaboration with world-renowned researchers, computational experts, and climate scientists at Cornell University.
  • Opportunities to shape the future of sustainable technology education by contributing directly to the innovative AI4S curriculum.
  • Access to state-of-the-art computational resources, research labs, and academic networks.
  • A platform to showcase your research globally and establish long-lasting international institutional partnerships.

Eligibility Criteria

Cornell University welcomes visionaries from around the globe who meet the following requirements:

  • Hold a current faculty position (Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor) at an accredited higher education institution worldwide.
  • A strong research track record in artificial intelligence, machine learning, environmental science, computational sustainability, or related disciplines.
  • A clear, compelling proposal demonstrating how your expertise will enrich the AI4S research initiative and curriculum.
  • Strong communication skills in English to engage effectively in collaborative research and university instruction.

How to Apply

Securing a visiting professorship at Cornell requires a thoughtful, well-prepared application. Be sure to submit all required documentation before the official deadline on October 15, 2026.

  • Research & Teaching Statement: Outline your proposed collaborative research projects and curriculum contributions.
  • Comprehensive CV: Highlight your major academic achievements, publication history, and research grants.
  • Letters of Recommendation: Provide professional references from recognized academic peers in your field.

Do not miss this transformative opportunity to redefine the role of technology in global sustainability at Cornell University. Take the next step in your academic journey and begin preparing your application today!
