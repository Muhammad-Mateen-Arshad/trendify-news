AAUW International Fellowships 2027 in the USA: Fully Funded STEM Scholarships for Women Worldwide - Global South Opportunities

Published by AI Pipeline | September 22, 2026

The AAUW International Fellowships 2027 in the United States offer a life-changing opportunity for exceptional women scholars around the world, with a special emphasis on applicants from Pakistan and across the Global South. Sponsored by the prestigious American Association of University Women, this program serves as a fully funded fellowship dedicated to empowering female leaders, researchers, and innovators pursuing graduate or postgraduate studies at top-tier U.S. institutions.

What is Included

This award provides fully funded financial support tailored to cover crucial academic and living expenses so you can focus entirely on your educational journey. Key coverage includes:

  • Substantial Academic Allowance: Fellowship stipends range from $20,000 for Master's degrees, $25,000 for Doctoral degrees, up to $50,000 for Postdoctoral research.
  • Tuition and Academic Fees: Comprehensive support covering tuition costs, textbooks, and essential research supplies.
  • Living Allowance: Financial assistance for monthly housing, living expenses, and child care costs.
  • International Leadership Network: Direct access to an esteemed global network of female scientists, innovators, and academic mentors.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for this empowering fellowship opportunity, candidates must fulfill the following requirements:

  • Target Applicants: Female scholars who hold citizenship in countries other than the United States (including Pakistani and international students).
  • Educational Background: Must hold an academic degree equivalent to a U.S. bachelor’s degree at the time of application.
  • STEM Focus & Field of Study: Open to all academic disciplines, with high priority given to women advancing in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) fields.
  • Return Commitment: Candidates must demonstrate a clear commitment to returning to their home country to contribute to societal development and mentor future generations of women.
  • English Proficiency: Demonstrated proficiency in English through recognized tests (e.g., TOEFL or IELTS).

How to Stand Out

As an international counselor, my top advice is to start preparing your essays early. Highlight not only your academic achievements and research ambitions but also your passion for driving community impact. Demonstrate clearly how studying in the United States will help you advance gender equality and scientific innovation upon returning home.
