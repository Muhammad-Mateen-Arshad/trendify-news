AI Scholarships in UK for Pakistani Students

Published on October 10, 2026

The University of Southampton has opened applications for a funded PhD opportunity titled "Deep Learning Modelling of Nano Defects in Chip Fabrication." This project combines semiconductor manufacturing, experimental characterisation, high-performance computing, and machine learning to forecast nanoscale defects during chip production.

Scholarship Overview

This research position focuses on using artificial intelligence to identify defect patterns in modern microprocessors, graphics chips, and memory devices before problems occur during fabrication. Successful candidates will undertake hands-on physical fabrication work—using methods such as plasma etching, electron-beam lithography, and material deposition—before applying deep learning algorithms to experimental data to improve manufacturing yield.

Who Can Apply

The opportunity is open to both domestic and international applicants, including students from Pakistan. Applicants must meet the following general criteria:

  • Hold at least a UK 2:1 honours degree or an equivalent international qualification. Specific academic subject requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Provide proof of English-language proficiency, depending on academic background.

Benefits

Funding details vary depending on student residency:

  • UK and EU students receive full tuition fee coverage along with a tax-free living stipend. Exact stipend values are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • International candidates, including Pakistani students, can apply, though full coverage of international tuition fees may depend on securing additional faculty funding to cover the difference between domestic and international rates.

How to Apply

Candidates interested in this opportunity must submit their application before the official closing date on June 1, 2027:

  • Apply online through the University of Southampton’s postgraduate research admissions system.
  • Choose the research route in the Faculty of Engineering and Physical Sciences and select the PhD in Electronic and Electrical Engineering.
  • Identify the specific project and supervisor on the application form.
  • Submit all required documentation, including a CV, research proposal, two academic references, degree certificates, and academic transcripts.
