Colorado nonprofit that gives scholarships to kids for private school set to expand to all states

Published on October 09, 2026

Scholarship Overview

ACE Scholarships is a Colorado-based non-profit organization founded in 2000 to help low-income students access better educational opportunities. Originally based in Greenwood Village and operating across 13 states—where it distributed nearly 17,000 awards in a single year—the organization is expanding its services to cover all 50 states. The organization maintains strong operational efficiency, ensuring that 90% of funds directly benefit students while 10% covers overhead and administrative costs. ACE Scholarships works in tandem with new federal initiatives, such as the Education Freedom Tax Credit program, to bridge financial gaps for families seeking primary and secondary educational choices.

Who Can Apply

Scholarship opportunities cater to a wide group of students nationwide:

  • Students attending private schools, public schools, charter schools, or homeschooling programs.
  • Low-income applicants as well as families earning up to 300% of the median income in their local area.
  • Additional eligibility criteria, such as grade level or GPA requirements, are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The program offers flexible funding options to make learning more affordable:

  • Average scholarship amounts of $3,500, which often cover one-third to one-half of a child's private school tuition.
  • Coverage for essential learning add-ons, including tutoring services, after-school initiatives, books, and basic school supplies.
  • Financial assistance for school fees, daily transportation, computer technology, internet access, and specialized support services for students with disabilities.

How to Apply

Applicants can submit their details through an automated digital system built by ACE Scholarships. The accessible platform accommodates families fluent in 90 different languages, allowing applicants to request financial aid and purchase necessary educational tools directly. A dedicated support team of more than 120 staff members is also being established to guide families and schools through the process. Official application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
