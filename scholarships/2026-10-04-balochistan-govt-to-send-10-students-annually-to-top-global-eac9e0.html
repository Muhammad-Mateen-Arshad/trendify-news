Balochistan Govt to Send 10 Students Annually to Top Global Universities on Fully Funded PhD Scholarships

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Government of Balochistan has launched a new overseas doctoral scholarship program through the Balochistan Education Endowment Fund (BEEF). Every year, 10 outstanding candidates from the province will be selected to pursue PhD studies at top international universities. Chief Minister Mir Sarfraz Bugti described the program as a major milestone aimed at connecting the youth of Balochistan with world-class education and research. According to BEEF Chief Executive Officer Khalid Mandai, the fund has previously awarded 23,520 scholarships across various academic levels, including BS, intermediate, college, ADP, ADE, DAE, matriculation district toppers, and Cadet Excellence schemes.

Who Can Apply

The program is intended for high-achieving students from Balochistan who wish to complete doctoral studies abroad.
