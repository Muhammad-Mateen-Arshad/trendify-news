Minister for Justice offers to prioritise Gaza students’ appeals over visa refusal

Published on October 06, 2026

Four Palestinian students from Gaza were selected for fully-funded scholarships to study at Irish higher education institutions, including an engineering program at Trinity College Dublin. Their visa applications were initially refused by Minister for Justice Jim O’Callaghan, partly because original documents were not submitted. Legal representatives argued that civilian infrastructure in Gaza, such as banks and post offices, no longer exists to obtain these files. The High Court, under Judge Anthony Barr, fast-tracked judicial review challenges regarding these refusals.

Scholarship Overview

The program offers fully-funded opportunities for students from Gaza to attend Irish universities. Recent High Court proceedings revealed that the Minister offered to fast-track the students' visa appeals to be decided by 5pm on Friday. Without this prioritised timeline, appeals could take over a year, preventing candidates from joining their academic programs for the current year.
