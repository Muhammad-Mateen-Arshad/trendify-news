Better Opportunities for Youth can Transform Balochistan’s Future

Published on October 09, 2026

The Chief Minister’s Merit Scholarships for Leading PhD Programs (CMSPP) operates under the Shaheed Benazir Bhutto Fully Funded Scholarship Program and is administered by the Balochistan Education Endowment Fund (BEEF). For the 2027 session, the application window is open to provide qualified students from Balochistan the opportunity to pursue higher research and doctoral studies at top global institutions, helping to enhance the academic, technical, and socio-economic growth of the province.

Scholarship Overview

The CMSPP initiative aims to fund doctoral studies specifically at QS World Top 100 universities. By offering complete financial support, the scholarship gives scholars access to advanced research, new technologies, and intellectual capital. Scholars can apply these skills upon returning to strengthen Balochistan's medical, educational, technological, and governmental infrastructure.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must meet the following baseline conditions:

  • Be an eligible student from Balochistan seeking to enroll in a PhD program.
  • Independently secure admission to a university ranked in the QS World Top 100.
  • Specific age restrictions, academic grades, or general eligibility criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The allocation of available scholarship seats is divided by academic discipline as follows:

  • 70% of available seats are allocated to STEM fields.
  • 30% of available seats are allocated to the social sciences, arts, and humanities.

Benefits

The CMSPP is a fully funded scholarship program. Successful candidates receive coverage for:

  • Full educational costs
  • Living expenses
  • Health insurance
  • Round-trip airfare
  • Visa fees

How to Apply

Candidates must take the following steps to compete for the scholarship:

  • Apply directly to any QS World Top 100 university and secure an admission offer, as BEEF does not provide assistance with university admissions.
  • Undertake and pass an aptitude test administered by BEEF to evaluate applicants on a merit basis.

Exact submission procedures, portal details, and application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
