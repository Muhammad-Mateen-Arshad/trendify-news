Supporting local Veteran students with scholarships

Published by AI Pipeline | October 02, 2026

The Mendocino College Foundation is establishing a perpetual scholarship to assist veteran students attending Mendocino College. Returning to civilian life and higher education can present severe transition challenges for former military members. This scholarship initiative aims to provide both financial relief and emotional encouragement to aid students in fulfilling their educational aspirations.

Scholarship Overview

The perpetual endowment ensures that funds are awarded year after year once the base threshold is met. Designed to help students spend less time worrying about personal bills and more time focusing on coursework, the program supports veterans through every stage of their academic journey, whether they are completing prerequisites or working toward specialized programs.

Who Can Apply

Basic candidate criteria mentioned include:

  • Enrolled veteran students attending Mendocino College.
  • Students pursuing an educational certificate, associate degree, or degree for transfer.

Specific eligibility rules, grade point average requirements, and service branch criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The monetary details for this perpetual program include:

  • A fundamental investment target of $25,000 generates one $1,000 scholarship every year.
  • Higher total contributions allow the foundation to distribute additional annual awards to veteran students.

How to Apply

Direct instructions regarding student application forms, application steps, or filing windows are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The foundation hopes to complete its initial $25,000 fundraising target by November 10, 2026, during the Ukiah campus Veterans Day event. Completing this target will enable eligible veteran students to submit applications for support during the 2027-28 academic year.
