Rare Scholarship Funds Specialist Inclusive Education Training

Published on October 08, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Dr Coral Kemp Scholarship offers a rare professional development pathway for an early childhood educator looking to specialize in inclusive education. Established by Specialist Teaching and Research (STaR) in partnership with the Institute of Special Educators (InSpEd) and the University of Newcastle, this program prepares teachers to support children with disabilities and developmental delays. The award honors Dr Coral Kemp OAM, STaR's Founding Director, who established the not-for-profit organization in 2001 and has dedicated over 30 years to special education and research.

Benefits

Recipients of this scholarship receive a comprehensive funding and employment package combining academic study with paid practical work, including:

  • Full postgraduate tuition coverage over two years for the Master of Special and Inclusive Education (Early Childhood) at the University of Newcastle.
  • Paid employment for two to three days per week with STaR's Early Childhood Specialist Education team while studying.
  • Professional mentoring and practical guidance from experienced Special Educators.
  • A clear career pathway into a specialist role using evidence-based and family-centered early intervention approaches.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible for this funding opportunity, candidates must fulfill the following entry criteria:

  • Be an Australian citizen or a permanent resident.
  • Hold an early childhood teaching qualification recognized by ACECQA.
  • Possess relevant professional experience in the field.
  • Meet the entry requirements for admission into the Master of Special and Inclusive Education at the University of Newcastle.

How to Apply

Applications close at 5pm on Thursday, 22 October 2026. Specific application steps and submission procedures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
