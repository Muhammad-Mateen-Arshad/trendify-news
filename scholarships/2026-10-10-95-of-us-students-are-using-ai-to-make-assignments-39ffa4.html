95% of US Students Are Using AI to Make Assignments

Published on October 10, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The provided source primarily discusses trends in academic artificial intelligence (AI) usage rather than specific scholarship program details. Based on a Clarivate survey of more than 500 graduate and undergraduate students, 95 percent of students use AI tools for their projects and academic assignments. This represents a substantial rise from 45 percent in 2024. The survey notes that this statistic does not imply students are relying on chatbots to complete entire papers on their own.

Students reported using AI for specific academic tasks, including:

  • Summarizing articles (61 percent)
  • Brainstorming research concepts (61 percent)
  • Editing and writing academic papers (51 percent)

The survey also highlighted issues with accuracy, noting that users encountered fake citations and incorrect data. Verification is common, with 60 percent of undergraduates and 64 percent of graduate students stating they fact-check AI results most or all of the time. Additionally, separate research conducted by the University of Copenhagen evaluating 27 language models revealed that chatbot responses offered less information diversity than traditional search engine results.
