JI Launches Campaign for Free Education for Every Child

Published on October 10, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Jamaat-e-Islami (JI) chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman has announced a nationwide educational campaign demanding free, quality education for every child in Pakistan. Highlighting access to schooling as a fundamental right, the party aims to address growing educational gaps amid economic hardship, rising inflation, and high utility costs across the country. According to party statistics, millions of children currently remain out of school, including 5 million in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, over 11.8 million in Punjab, and more than 7 million in Sindh.

To help address these issues, Jamaat-e-Islami operates the Bano Qabil programme, an initiative designed to equip youth with modern education and technical skill training. Through nationwide initiatives, particularly within Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, over 1.7 million children have received training supported by fully funded scholarships. Additionally, the party is working to support Gaza students in continuing their studies, including medical programs.

Who Can Apply

The skill development programs and free education campaigns target youth and children across Pakistan, with significant activity in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Benefits

Participants in the Bano Qabil training initiative receive free instruction in professional skills and modern educational topics. Key benefits reported include:

  • Fully funded scholarships for youth development, benefiting over 1.7 million children so far.
  • Instruction focused on practical professional skills and modern learning.
  • Educational assistance for Gaza students striving to complete medical and university studies.
