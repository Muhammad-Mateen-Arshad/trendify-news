Fi Europe 2026: Discover a world of health and functional ingredients – all in one place

Published on October 09, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Fi Europe 2026 will take place in Frankfurt from 17 to 19 November 2026, featuring over 1,650 exhibitors and an expected 27,000 attendees from more than 135 countries.
  • The event includes a specialized Health Ingredients area focusing on components aimed at physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing, including healthy ageing, cognitive function, metabolic health, immunity, and digestive wellness.
  • Conference topics will address developments in nutrition, including protein and fibre fortification, gut-brain health, nutrient density, personalised nutrition, prebiotics, probiotics, postbiotics, and the impact of GLP-1 medications.

What the Source Says

According to information from event organizers, Fi Europe 2026 brings together research teams, manufacturers, and ingredient suppliers to explore formulation solutions and nutritional trends. The exhibition features a wide range of ingredients, including vitamins, minerals, amino acids, proteins, botanicals, bioactives, dietary fibres, prebiotics, probiotics, extracts, fungi, and yeasts.

Formulators attend the event to address challenges related to balancing nutritional value, taste, texture, stability, cost, and clean-label expectations. Sessions will feature speakers from industry research groups such as Mintel, Innova Market Insights, Euromonitor International, Everest Group, and The Protein Brewery. Specific conference themes cover weight management strategies, metabolic health, gut-brain connections, and new product innovations. Additional activities include an Innovation Hub, a New Product Zone, and dedicated networking events.

Practical Notes

Attendees can save up to €232 on ticket prices prior to an advance price increase scheduled for 19 October at 5pm CEST. Specific details regarding personal medical applications or individualized health recommendations are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Always talk to your doctor before making changes to your health routine or dietary intake.
