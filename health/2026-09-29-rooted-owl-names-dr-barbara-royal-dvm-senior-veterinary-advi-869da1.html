What the Source Says

According to an announcement from Rooted Owl, integrative veterinarian Dr. Barbara Royal has joined the company to lead nutrition strategy and guide clinical study design. Dr. Royal is an owner and CEO of Animal Diet Formulator and the College of Integrative Veterinary Therapies. In her new role, she will review upcoming formulations, including an upcoming line of 12 cat wellness products featuring items such as Cardio Support and Kidney Support.

Rooted Owl states it has completed studies on selected existing products and maintains NASC certification.

Rooted Owl products are distributed through Amazon, Chewy, Petco Mexico, independent retailers, rootedowl.com, and partners across the United States and Latin America.
