Netflix Sets November 2026 Release Date for New Medical Drama ‘Always on Call’

Published on October 06, 2026

Netflix has announced the global release date for its upcoming Norwegian Original series, Always on Call. The seven-part show is scheduled to launch worldwide on November 26, 2026, marking the first medical drama title from Netflix Nordics.

About the series

Always on Call was created and written by Harald Rosenløw-Eeg and Jan Trygve Røyneland, with Stian Kristiansen and Liv Karin Dahlstrøm directing. The writing team also features Ane Isungset, Maren Skolem, Stian Kristiansen, Liv Karin Dahlstrøm, and Thorkild Schrumpf. The series is produced by studio Klynge, with Magnus Ramsdalen, Esben Bundgaard-Jørgensen Selvig, and Kaare Daniel Steen serving as producers. Specific episode runtimes are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Sara Khorami stars in the lead role of surgeon Sofia Karimi. Key members of the cast include:

  • Thorbjørn Harr as Anders Platou
  • Jan Gunnar Røise as Stig Solvang
  • Silje Storstein as Renate
  • Alfred Ekker Strande as Herbert
  • Zoe Mæland Rodgers as Thea

The wider cast also features Ola G. Furuseth, Nader Khademi, John Emil Jørgensrud, Eili Harboe, Ellen Bendu, Haakon Aunlie, Tora Dietrichson, and William Cyrus Estabraghi.

What to expect

The plot centers on an intense emergency room where exhausted medical staff treat every patient equally, regardless of their social standing or profession. Sofia, a highly talented surgeon who lacks patience, is assigned to this chaotic ER to salvage her career. Navigating low staffing, hospital politics, and demanding cases, she encounters initial resistance that gradually turns into unexpected warmth, romance, and community. Facing a critical personal crossroads, Sofia must decide whether to continue her ambition alone or embrace her colleagues as a true family.
