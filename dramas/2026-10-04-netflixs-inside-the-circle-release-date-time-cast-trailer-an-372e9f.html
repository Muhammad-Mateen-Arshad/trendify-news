Netflix’s ‘Inside the Circle’ Release Date, Time, Cast, Trailer and Plot of the Mexcan Thriller That Turns a Leaked Video Into a Friendship-Wrecking Story

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

What's new

Netflix is expanding its international slate with the upcoming Mexican suspense series Inside the Circle, titled El círculo in Spanish. Following the release of its first promotional preview on September 9, all eight episodes are scheduled to land simultaneously on October 7 across more than 190 countries. Although most coverage confirms October 7, a single early review noted an October 6 debut.

Regarding the exact release time on launch day, it is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The thriller centers on four long-time friends whose personal lives unravel after a leaked video threatens to reveal their secrets, fears, and ambitions. The plot quickly escalates around the murder of a famous actress, pulling the group into a high-stakes web of espionage and blackmail.

The series adapts Jorge Zepeda Patterson's political novel Los corruptores, with Zepeda Patterson acting as a consultant. While the book focused on political critique in 2013 Mexico, the television adaptation prioritizes the interpersonal drama among the core friends. Production and writing details include:

  • Direction: Directed by Alejandro Lozano.
  • Writing: Scripted by Simona Horcha, Fanie Soto, Fernando Rovzar, Fabián Archondo, and Alejandro Lozano.
  • Production: Produced by Lemon Studios. Producer Billy Rovzar stated that Francisco Ramos initially brought the project forward prior to becoming a Netflix executive.

What to expect

The production relies on a recognizable ensemble to drive its narrative tension:

  • Main Cast: Zuria Vega, Michel Brown, Raúl Briones, and Osvaldo Benavides.
  • Supporting Cast: Jorge Zárate, Natalia Coronado, Mariané Cartas, Diego Sandoval, and Saúl Lisazo.

Early critical feedback from But Why Tho based on the first three episodes highlighted a strong setup for the main group and its surrounding mysteries, noting that the show's success will depend on how cleanly it resolves its various plot threads.
