Mark Rober Talks New Netflix Series ‘Schooled’ and Why He Partnered with the Streamer

Published on October 04, 2026

Former NASA engineer and YouTube creator Mark Rober recently shared insights into his expanding partnership with Netflix during the Bloomberg ScreenTime conference in Los Angeles. First partnering with the streaming service in August 2025, Rober is set to launch an unscripted competition show titled Schooled! in early 2027.

What's new

The ongoing partnership between Netflix and Rober's CrunchLabs brand has already generated over 104 million viewing hours across titles like Mark Rober’s CrunchLabs, Elmo and Mark Rober’s Merry Giftmas, and Camp CrunchLabs. The next major milestone for the brand is Schooled!, a high-stakes project produced in partnership with Jimmy Kimmel’s production company, Kimmelot.

About the series

The show turns an actual school campus into an engineering playground, featuring Rober in the role of principal. Key elements of the project include:

  • Fourteen contestants, aged 9 to 17, divided into seven teams of two.
  • Practical science and engineering challenges, such as launching trash can rockets off the roof onto a basketball court, placing trampolines in hallways, and filling lockers with gel.
  • A grand prize featuring half a million dollars for college and an apprenticeship with Rober at CrunchLabs.
  • A large production scale involving approximately 300 set workers daily.

What to expect

Unlike traditional network formats, Schooled! aims to maintain direct-to-camera engagement and break the fourth wall to preserve authentic online energy. Rober emphasized that joining Netflix provides the upfront capital necessary for large-scale builds while enabling CrunchLabs to expand across multiple platforms alongside his regular YouTube work.
