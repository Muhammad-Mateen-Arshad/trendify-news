Dragon Ball Super: Beerus Release Schedule: When Does Episode 1 Release?

Published on October 10, 2026

Anime fans have an exciting release to look forward to as a refreshed edition of a popular storyline prepares to launch globally. Dragon Ball Super: Beerus brings back early chapters of the anime with reconstructed storytelling, newly added scenes, redrawn sequences, and enhanced visuals.

What's new

This project revisits familiar events with a modern presentation across a reported total of 19 episodes. Rather than launching a completely original adventure, the series upgrades the initial major story arcs, providing fans with a fresh visual experience for classic battles.

About the series

The narrative centers on Goku encountering a formidable opponent in Beerus, the God of Destruction. Unlike typical villains, Beerus possesses the power to erase entire worlds. After waking from a long slumber, Beerus searches for the Super Saiyan God, eventually crossing paths with Goku. Storyline highlights include:

  • The introduction of Whis, who possesses remarkable abilities and a calm personality.
  • The reigniting of a personal rivalry during the Golden Frieza arc.
  • The expansion of the universe and new fighters featured in the Universe 6 Tournament arc.

What to expect

The broadcast begins in Japan on Fuji TV and affiliated channels on October 11, 2026, at 11:15 PM JST. Streaming availability differs across platforms and regions:

  • Crunchyroll: International streaming starts on October 11, 2026. The exact release time is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Netflix US: Streaming begins on October 17, 2026, following a weekly rollout.
  • Japanese Streaming Services: Prime Video and U-NEXT start streaming on October 12 at 12:15 AM JST, while other local platforms will carry episodes on Thursdays at 12:15 AM JST.

Assuming weekly releases continue without delays or breaks, the final episode is projected to air in February 2027.
