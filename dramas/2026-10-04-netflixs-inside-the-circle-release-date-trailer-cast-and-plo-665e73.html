Netflix’s ‘Inside the Circle’ Release Date, Trailer, Cast and Plot Explained

Published by AI Pipeline | October 04, 2026

What's new

Netflix has announced the global premiere of its upcoming Mexican thriller, Inside the Circle (titled El círculo in Spanish). The worldwide launch is set for October 7, 2026, across more than 190 countries, although one early review lists October 6 as the start date. A first trailer running one minute and fifty-one seconds debuted on September 9. As for the precise release time and total episode count, that is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The thriller is based on the book Los corruptores by author Jorge Zepeda Patterson, who joined the project as a consultant. Produced by Lemon Studios, the adaptation was penned by Simona Horcha, Fanie Soto, Fernando Rovzar, Fabián Archondo, and Alejandro Lozano. Lozano also co-directs the series alongside Bernardo de la Rosa.

The story begins when a video regarding a recently deceased actress named Pamela Dosantos is shared by Tomas. Because the clip implicates one of Mexico's most powerful political figures, it sparks extortion and pushes four lifelong friends into a dangerous situation. The central cast includes:

  • Michel Brown as Tomas
  • Raúl Briones as Mario
  • Osvaldo Benavides as Jaime
  • Zuria Vega as Amelia

The broader ensemble cast features Jorge Zárate, Natalia Coronado, Mariané Cartas, Diego Sandoval, Saúl Lisazo, María Elisa Camargo, and Héctor Molina.

What to expect

Described by Netflix as gritty, suspenseful, and political, the narrative focuses on conspiracy, criminal impunity, and the fragile nature of personal loyalty. An early review of the first three episodes from But Why Tho assigned the opening arc an 8 out of 10 score, praising the balance between the group's personal bonds and the larger political mystery, while questioning whether every storyline can be fully wrapped up.
