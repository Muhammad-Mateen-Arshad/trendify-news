8 new TV shows to watch this weekend on Netflix, Prime Video, and HBO (October 9-11)

Published on October 09, 2026

What's new

A fresh lineup of television series is heading to streaming platforms, bringing a wide range of genres including horror adaptations, sci-fi animation, workplace dramas, and supernatural comedies. Audiences can look forward to upcoming premieres across platforms such as Netflix and Prime Video.

About the series

Several notable shows are joining the streaming schedule:

  • Carrie: Helmed by Mike Flanagan, this eight-episode Prime Video series updates Stephen King's novel for the Gen-Z era. Summer H. Howell stars as Carrie, alongside Samantha Sloyan as her mother Margaret White, and Alison Thornton as Chris Hargensen.
  • Below: A Canadian thriller centered on a small coastal town. Josh Hartnett stars as a fisherman working alongside a marine researcher, played by Mackenzie Davis, to safeguard his community from a sea creature. Showrunner Jesse McKeown cited John Carpenter films and Jaws as major influences.
  • NCIS New York: This franchise spin-off features Sam Hanna (LL Cool J), previously seen in NCIS: Los Angeles, as he moves to New York to join the local NCIS team. Scott Caan and Jennifer Beals also join the cast.
  • Eternally Yours: A comedy about Charles (Ed Weeks) and Liz (Allegra Edwards), a vampire couple whose 500-year marriage has hit a rut. Their dynamic is tested when their daughter (Helen J. Shen) introduces her human boyfriend (Jaren Lewison).
  • Avatar: Seven Havens: Set about 225 years after The Legend of Korra, this animated story follows Pavi (Saheli Khan), a young earthbender in a post-apocalyptic cyberpunk world who discovers she is the new Avatar.
  • Haunted Hotel: An adult animated comedy on Netflix created by Matt Roller. It follows Katherine (Eliza Coupe), a single mother who inherits The Undervale hotel, which is haunted by her late brother Nathan (Will Forte).
  • Cupertino: Created by legal drama veterans Michelle and Robert King, this series examines Silicon Valley culture through two lawyers, played by Mike Colter and Rachel Keller, who run a firm.

What to expect

Audiences can expect a mix of modern high-school horror, futuristic cyberpunks with hoverboards, and relationship comedies. Specific broadcast dates and release schedules for every show are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
