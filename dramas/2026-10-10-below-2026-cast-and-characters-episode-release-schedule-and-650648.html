Below (2026) Cast and Characters, Episode Release Schedule and Where to Watch

Published on October 10, 2026

Netflix has released the supernatural thriller series Below, created by Jesse McKeown. The six-episode limited series combines elements of creature horror, mystery, and family drama, offering viewers a story centered on survival, community ties, and a mysterious threat from the ocean.

What's new

All six episodes of Below premiered globally on Netflix on October 8, 2026. Because the show was released as a full limited series, audiences can watch the complete storyline immediately without waiting for weekly releases. Specific episode titles and individual plot summaries are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The narrative takes place in Snook’s Arm, a remote coastal fishing town in Newfoundland, Canada. The plot focuses on Calvin Penney, a single father of three who strives to keep his family together and protect his hometown. The townspeople are already confronting difficult choices regarding whether to accept a buyout and leave their homes. The situation becomes far more dangerous when a mysterious sea creature arrives and threatens their way of life.

To examine the strange events linked to the water, Calvin collaborates with marine researcher Dr. Fonda Howander. Filmed in Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada, the production utilizes the local rugged coastline and isolated geography to establish its atmospheric setting.

What to expect

The drama features an ensemble cast dealing with both internal family friction and the mysterious danger from the sea:

  • Josh Hartnett as Calvin Penney, a fisherman determined to guard his town and family despite past personal misfortunes.
  • Mackenzie Davis as Dr. Fonda Howander, a marine researcher assisting with the scientific investigation.
  • Charlie Heaton as Wade Penney, Calvin's oldest son who remains in the community and experiences disturbing dreams.
  • Additional key roles played by Rohan Campbell, Ruby Stokes, Kaleb Horn, Willow Kean, Natasha Henstridge, David Wenham, and Alexander Karim.

Below is available to stream exclusively on Netflix for platform subscribers.
