From an artistic perspective, Kawauchi's projects combine static photography with video recorded on a tripod to capture two distinct time axes simultaneously.

Her visual themes balance soft light with the coexistence of life and decay. Her subjects include ordinary moments—such as light reflecting on spoons, moisture on plants, and frying eggs—placed alongside images of mortality like animal remains, discarded shells, and rising ash.
