‘Not a Stranger’ Release Date, Cast, Trailer, Plot and Everything Else You Need to Know About Netflix’s Creepy New Nanny Thriller

Published by AI Pipeline | October 03, 2026

What's new

Netflix is adding a new Turkish psychological thriller to its international lineup with the upcoming series Not a Stranger, scheduled to premiere on September 17, 2026. Produced in Turkey by Bombon Studios, the show explores how a quiet household unravels after a new helper is brought into the home. Netflix has released a full trailer running just over a minute and a half that gives audiences a first look at the show's suspense. Specific details regarding the total episode count are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Created and written by Tuğba Doğan and directed by Mert Baykal, the Turkish-language series carries the original title Seni Tanıyorum, which translates roughly to "I Know You." The story focuses on Funda, a painter who pauses her career after giving birth to a newborn baby girl. Torn between motherhood and her art, Funda decides to accept help and hires a nanny named Nazlı. While Nazlı initially seems like a solution, her mysterious behavior and hidden secrets soon spark tension and endanger the family.

The core lineup includes:

  • Elçin Sangu as Funda
  • Ozan Dolunay as Funda's husband, İlker
  • Melis Sezen as Nazlı, the new nanny

The broader ensemble cast features Cemal Hünal, Efe Taşdelen, Murat Garipağaoğlu, Mine Teber, Nergis Öztürk, Arbil Tabur, Esra Şahin, along with Ada and Arya Özdemir.

What to expect

The series carries a TV-MA rating due to its focus on domestic danger, psychological manipulation, mature relationship dynamics, sensuality, coarse language, smoking, and drinking. Because of these mature themes and situations involving risk to a child, it is not considered suitable for younger viewers. Instead of relying on jump scares, the plot builds slow-burn dread around manipulation, trust, and family conflict, delivering a psychological thriller focused on an invited stranger with hidden motives.
