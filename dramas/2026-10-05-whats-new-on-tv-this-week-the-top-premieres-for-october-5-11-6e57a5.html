What’s New on TV This Week? The Top Premieres for October 5-11, 2026

Published on October 05, 2026

What's new

The broadcast and streaming lineup for October 5-11, 2026, brings a wide selection of premieres across traditional television networks and digital platforms. Viewers can look forward to new releases on CBS, Netflix, Prime Video, HBO Max, Paramount+, MGM+, Investigation Discovery, and Food Network. The schedule features a mix of scripted dramas, supernatural comedies, animated series, true crime documentaries, and competition shows.

About the series

Several high-profile dramatic projects are leading the week's debuts. Prime Video releases an updated adaptation of Stephen King's Carrie at 3:00am ET, starring Summer H. Howell as Carrie White and Samantha Sloyan as her mother, Margaret. The story centers on Carrie entering public high school following her father's death, dealing with severe social media bullying, and discovering telekinetic powers.

On CBS, NCIS: New York premieres at 9:00pm ET. The series sees Sam Hanna (LL COOL J) relocate to the New York field office to co-lead a team alongside a roguish partner portrayed by Scott Caan. CBS is also debuting Cupertino at 10:00pm ET, a legal drama featuring Mike Colter as an attorney cheated out of his tech start-up stock options who teams up with another attorney, played by Rachel Keller, to battle Silicon Valley executives.

Streaming options include Netflix's Below at 3:00am ET, starring Josh Hartnett as a fisherman protecting his town from a dangerous sea creature. Additionally, Netflix premieres the K-Drama rom-com Take Charge of My Heart at 4:00pm ET, following a corporate heir with an artificial heart who connects with an electric woman. Total episode counts for these new dramas are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The upcoming schedule also includes various genre projects across multiple networks:

  • Eternally Yours (CBS, 8:30pm ET): Ed Weeks and Allegra Edwards star as a vampire couple living in Seattle facing marital trouble after 500 years together.
  • Treasure Island (MGM+, 10:00pm ET): Tom Sweet, Hayley Atwell, and David Oyelowo star in an adaptation of the classic novel involving a race for Captain Flint's treasure.
  • Avatar: Seven Havens (Paramount+, 3:00am ET): A 2-D animated series following a new Earthbender Avatar after Korra who is viewed as a destroyer.
  • The Serial Killer Connection (ID, 9:00pm ET): A three-part series featuring Bonnie Lou and survivor Kara Robinson Chamberlain.
  • The Pet Cemetery Murders: A Ghost Story (HBO Max, 3:00am ET): A documentary centered on a 1985 bayou murder investigation.
  • Sweet Deceit (Food Network, 10:00pm ET): Hosted by Josh Peck, three-person teams play four rounds identifying disguised desserts for a $12,000 grand prize.
