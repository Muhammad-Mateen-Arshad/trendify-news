Okayama/Kurashiki: A Trip to Visit Bookstores and Art Museums

Published on October 06, 2026

What's new

A travel report details a two-day, one-night journey to Okayama and Kurashiki taken at the end of September. Among the cultural stops mentioned is the Orient Museum, which featured a special exhibition titled Stories of Ancient Gold. The location houses a museum cafe named Ibrik, where visitors can sample Turkish coffee made by boiling coffee grounds in a small pot and engage in coffee fortune-telling by flipping the cup onto its saucer.

What to expect

The travel account outlines several specific stops and attractions throughout the trip:

  • 451 Books, an independent bookstore reachable by bus from Okayama Station, featuring two floors, a spiral staircase, foreign books, and zines.
  • The local government-operated Orient Museum, displaying ancient excavated jewelry and gold crafts dating back to before the Common Era.
  • Omote-machi Book Square, a shopping arcade hosting four bookstores—including Agedor Galerie d'art—as part of a 2025 area revitalization project.
  • The Bikan Historical Quarter in Kurashiki, featuring evening projection mapping and illuminations.
  • The Ohara Museum of Art, housing works by artists such as Monet, Gauguin, Renoir, Degas, Picasso, Chagall, Puvis de Chavannes, Frédéric, and El Greco's The Annunciation.
