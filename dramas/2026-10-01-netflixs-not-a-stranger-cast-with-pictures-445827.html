Netflix’s ‘Not a Stranger’ Cast (with Pictures)

Published by AI Pipeline | October 01, 2026

What's new

Netflix is adding a new Turkish psychological thriller to its platform with the upcoming series 'Not a Stranger'. The show brings an intense storyline focused on desire, secrets, and complex relationship dynamics. Details regarding the exact release date and episode count are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The plot centers on a dedicated painter named Funda who decides to step back into her artistic career after having a baby. To allow herself time to focus on her work again, she hires a new caregiver for her newborn. However, the arrival of this nanny introduces unforeseen disruption and deep psychological tension into the family's home.

What to expect

The thriller brings together a notable ensemble of Turkish stars to portray its central characters:

  • Elçin Sangu plays Funda, an artist who faces growing suspicion as the story unfolds. Sangu is widely known for starring as Defne in 'Kiralık Aşk', as well as roles in 'Çarpışma' and 'Çöp Adam'.
  • Melis Sezen stars as Nazlı, the mysterious nanny who disrupts the home. Sezen previously gained recognition in 'Sadakatsiz', 'Gülcemal', and the movie 'Bizim İçin Şampiyon'.
  • Ozan Dolunay takes on the role of İlker, Funda's husband whose marriage faces tests and secret loyalty struggles. His past work includes 'Zalim İstanbul', 'Tatlı Küçük Yalancılar', 'Bizi Ayıran Çizgi', and 'Yüksek Sosyete'.
  • Cemal Hünal portrays Kemal, a character tied into the family's complex personal dynamic. Viewers may recognize him from 'Issız Adam', 'Diriliş Ertuğrul', 'Asi', and 'Sadakatsiz'.
  • Efe Taşdelen plays Emre, rounding out the secondary network of characters. His recent credits include 'Kızılcık Şerbeti' and 'Duy Beni'.
