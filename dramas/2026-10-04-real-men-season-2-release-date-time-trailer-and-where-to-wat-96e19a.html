‘Real Men’ Season 2 Release Date, Time, Trailer and Where to Watch as the Series Returns with a Bigger Cast and Messier Friendships

Published on October 04, 2026

What's new

The Italian comedy series 'Real Men' returns to Netflix for its second season, which is scheduled to premiere on Wednesday, October 7, 2026. Netflix is releasing all the new episodes at once for a full binge release. However, the exact premiere time is Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Furthermore, the total episode count for the new season is Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Netflix Italia shared the official trailer on August 27 alongside an English-language version released on the same day. Netflix also released a special video when filming began to mark the start of production.

About the series

The narrative centers on four lifelong friends attempting to manage modern relationships, careers, and dating. The story originally began when the men reluctantly participated in a workshop designed to help them unlearn traditional ideas regarding manhood.

What to expect

The upcoming season brings reversed relationship dynamics, sharp social satire, and paradoxical scenarios. Viewers will see the four core couples placed in situations that are the exact opposite of where they were left at the end of the first season. Even though shifts in status and personal lives throw off their search for gender balance, the four men remain closer friends than ever despite feeling out of place.

The core group returns with Maurizio Lastrico as Mattia, Matteo Martari as Massimo, Francesco Montanari as Riccardo, and Pietro Sermonti as Luigi. Returning cast members also include Thony, Sarah Felberbaum, Laura Adriani, Alice Lupparelli, Nicole Grimaudo, and Davide Iacopini.

The second season introduces several new actors to the group:

  • Carolina Crescentini
  • Silvia D’Amico
  • Alessio Boni
  • Giancarlo Commare
  • Ilenia Pastorelli

Behind the scenes, Matteo Oleotto and Milena Cocozza direct the season, with screenplays written by Furio Andreotti, Giulia Calenda, and Ugo Ripamonti. The series is produced by Groenlandia, with Matteo Rovere acting as producer.
