Netflix Is Officially Bringing Back This 21-Year-Old Crime Franchise

Published by AI Pipeline | September 28, 2026

A long-running Turkish crime-comedy franchise is expanding to television with the upcoming Netflix release Money Trap: The Karun Treasure. Set to premiere on October 16, 2026, the eight-episode production marks the first time the franchise has been adapted into a TV show format.

What's new

The franchise originally began in 2005 with the film Organize İşler (released as Magic Carpet Ride in the US), written and directed by Yılmaz Erdoğan. A sequel, Organize İşler Sazan Sarmalı (Money Trap), followed in 2019. The upcoming 2026 project brings the crew back to the screen, serving as both a reboot and a sequel within a universe familiar to long-time viewers.

About the series

Directed by Şenol Sönmez and produced by Necati Akpınar, Money Trap: The Karun Treasure combines con-artist comedy, action, and suspense across the streets and underground passages of Istanbul. The narrative kicks off when legendary scam artist Asım Noyan acquires a mythical map tied to the Treasure of Karun and attempts one final con before stepping away from his criminal life. This sets off a chaotic race involving Asım's crew, ruthless competitors, and international treasure hunters.

The series features an ensemble cast that includes:

  • Yılmaz Erdoğan (as Asım Noyan)
  • Kıvanç Tatlıtuğ
  • Ezgi Mola
  • Uraz Kaygılaroğlu
  • Rıza Kocaoğlu
  • Ersin Korkut
  • Okan Çabalar
  • Bensu Soral Baş
  • Atakan Çelik
  • Şükran Ovalı
  • Görkem Sevindik
  • Demet Akbağ

What to expect

The eight-episode series is designed to appeal to both long-time fans and international audiences discovering the franchise for the first time. Viewers do not need to have watched the prior two films to follow the storyline, though existing fans will experience a dose of nostalgia as returning characters assemble for Asım's ultimate score.
