Ben Affleck’s New Netflix Thriller Officially Arrives October 9

Published on October 06, 2026

Streaming giant Netflix is preparing for another high-profile project as the thriller film Animals officially debuts on Friday, October 9. The platform continues to expand its lineup with notable titles, including War Machine starring Alan Ritchson, Apex starring Charlize Theron, and the animated feature Ray Gunn directed by Brad Bird, which arrives on December 18.

What's new

The upcoming release marks Ben Affleck's return to direct a high-octane movie for the first time since 2016's Live By Night, following his 2023 drama Air. In addition to helming and starring in the project, Affleck authored the script together with co-writers Billy Ray and Connor O. McIntyre.

About the series

Affleck leads a prominent ensemble cast for this project, featuring several major television and film actors:

  • Ray Fisher, who co-starred with Affleck in Justice League as Cyborg.
  • Emmy winner Kerry Washington in a key role.
  • Steven Yeun, best known for portraying Glen in The Walking Dead.
  • Gillian Anderson, recognized for her role as Dana in The X-Files.

Specific character names for the supporting ensemble are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The storyline revolves around a candidate for mayor in Los Angeles and his wife after their son is kidnapped. As the couple scrambles to gather the necessary ransom payment, they become entangled in a web of deceit and must face decisions that could destroy their world.

Viewers seeking critical ratings will need to wait briefly. The film does not currently have a score on Rotten Tomatoes, as reviews from critics will begin arriving once the press embargo lifts later in the week. Audience scores on the Popcornmeter are expected to populate near the end of the weekend.
