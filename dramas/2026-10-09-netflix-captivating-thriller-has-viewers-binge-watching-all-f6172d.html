Netflix 'captivating' thriller has viewers binge-watching all 6 episodes in 1 day

Published on October 09, 2026

What's new

Netflix has expanded its streaming catalog with the release of Below, a six-part limited series that is already compelling audiences to binge-watch the entire season in a single day. The show enters the platform's library alongside other suspenseful thrillers like Dept. Q and The Beast in Me.

About the series

Set within a remote Newfoundland village, the narrative follows Calvin Penney, a big-hearted yet stubborn fisherman and single father of three. Still haunted by the mysterious death of his father decades earlier, Calvin is forced into action when a violent sea creature begins attacking his town. To protect his family and expose the island's hidden secrets, he collaborates with Fonda Howander, a visiting marine biologist.

What to expect

The show blends local folklore, suspense, horror, and emotional humor while focusing on themes of family, community, and inevitable change. Exact episode runtimes are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The cast includes:

  • Josh Hartnett as Calvin Penney
  • Charlie Heaton as Wade Penney
  • Mackenzie Davis as Fonda Howander
  • Willow Kean as Ruth Penney
  • Ruby Stokes as Mary Penney
  • Rohan Campbell as Jay Penney
  • Kaleb Horn as Noah

Reception

Audiences on social media have voiced strong enthusiasm for the series, with multiple viewers drawing comparisons to Apple TV's Widow's Bay. Critics have also responded positively. The Telegraph awarded the series four stars, highlighting its eerie atmosphere and genuine chills, while Variety commended it as a captivating mix of suspense and horror. All six episodes of Below are currently available to stream on Netflix.
