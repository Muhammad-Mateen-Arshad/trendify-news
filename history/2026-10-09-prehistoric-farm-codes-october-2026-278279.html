Prehistoric Farm codes (October 2026)

Published on October 09, 2026

The Discovery

In the digital experience Roblox Prehistoric Farm, virtual paleontologists travel between various islands to unearth buried fossilized bones. Once these remains are gathered and assembled, players can hatch and nurture the ancient creatures until they become companions that can be ridden. To assist in expanding collections, promotional codes offer extra resources for players embarking on these virtual expeditions. As of October 9, 2026, there are four active codes available for players to redeem.

What Scientists Found

Excavations require proper equipment and resources. Players use in-game coins to purchase upgraded shovels, fishing tools, bait, and faster marine vessels, which streamline the process of unearthing fossils and traveling across island waters. Additional rewards provide distinct advantages:

  • Luck boosts to increase the chances of locating rare fossil specimens.
  • Potions and exclusive items for collection expansion.
  • Special aquatic dinosaurs that function as alternatives to starter boats.

To successfully claim these rewards, players must first join the Project Mirvy Roblox group. After joining, the process requires navigating to the main menu, selecting the "CODES" option on the left side, and entering an active code into the text box. Specific details regarding real-world historical dates, dinosaur sizes, or scientific ages are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why It Matters

Future developments for the game are expected to introduce new islands, additional prehistoric creatures, or large-scale updates. Developer announcements regarding future code releases and game modifications are shared on the official Discord server, giving players direct updates on upcoming content additions.
