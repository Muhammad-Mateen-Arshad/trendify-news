Stunning Biblical Discoveries Emerge Near Jerusalem’s Temple Mount

Published on October 07, 2026

The Discovery

Archaeological excavations beneath Jerusalem’s City of David have exposed significant physical structures linked to biblical historical accounts. Work in the region uncovered an ancient stepped route leading toward the Temple Mount, along with a massive pool complex known as the Pool of Siloam. Infrastructure work revealed stone steps belonging to the pool, which holds religious importance as a destination visited by pilgrims traveling through the ancient city during the Second Temple period.

What Scientists Found

Excavations along the Pilgrimage Road and surrounding areas yielded a variety of everyday ancient objects alongside major structural remains. Key discoveries include:

  • Coins, commercial weights, and pottery along the stepped street, demonstrating that it functioned as a bustling commercial pathway lined with shops rather than solely a ceremonial route.
  • The Pool of Siloam complex, where stone steps and neighboring ancient reservoirs connected to historical pilgrim routes were unearthed.
  • A small golden bell discovered near the Temple Mount that remains capable of ringing, calling to mind descriptions in Exodus regarding high priests' garments.

Why It Matters

These historical sites provide physical context for ancient scriptural writings, allowing modern visitors to walk the exact uphill paths taken by past worshippers. The physical climb along the Pilgrimage Road mirrors references to ascending toward Jerusalem found in the Songs of Ascents, such as Psalm 122. Observers, including U.S. Ambassador Mike Huckabee, note that these findings highlight the Jewish foundation of Western civilization and provide tangible proof of Jerusalem's long-standing ancient history.
